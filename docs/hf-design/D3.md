# D3: Failure-mode-first high-fidelity tracing for Flightline

Design for the `high-fidelity-traces` branch (from origin/feat/automatic-tracking-v2 at fc84dad). Philosophy: failure-mode first. Every failure mode in the catalog (section 1.2) gets either a mechanism that resolves it or a guard that abstains with a specific reason code, and the synthetic benchmark (section 9) has scenarios for each. All numbers marked E1 to E7 were measured in small feasibility experiments under `/tmp/flightline-understand/design/D3-scratch/` (index in Appendix A). The repo was not modified.

---

## 1. Summary and key ideas

**Summary (10 lines).**
1. Replace the per-frame heuristic linker, the v2 beam search, and the legacy-link fallback with an engine (`v3`) that runs entirely in the worker. It has three stages: a coarse whole-clip scan, a native-resolution launch-and-flight pass, and a physics fit with completion.
2. The worker decodes the file itself with WebCodecs and a dependency-free MP4/MOV demuxer, so it gets every frame exactly once with container timestamps. Element seeks to frame centers with rVFC are the fallback.
3. A launch exists only when a ball-like spot that sat still is permanently vacated, with a discontinuity, as the club passes. No vacate means no trace, which handles practice swings, whiffs, and extra balls.
4. The ball is the leading object that leaves that spot on a straight line, with constant or perspective-decaying speed and ball-sized width. Club heads fail straightness, size, and lead in both camera angles. No launch-angle gates.
5. Flight tracking uses native-resolution regions of interest (ROIs) placed by a physics prediction, with seven-frame track-before-detect (TBD) and empirically calibrated false-alarm thresholds. There is no "coaster".
6. One 3D ballistic model (drag, lift, spin-axis curve) through an estimated pinhole camera fits all observations robustly. The address ball's size fixes the scale and the slow-motion factor is fitted.
7. After the last confident sighting the same model completes the arc to landing, frame exit, or an uncertainty fade. Every point carries `observed`, `interpolated`, `predicted`, or `launch`.
8. Abstention is strict: `tracked` requires a confirmed launch, a confirmed identity, and confidence ≥ 0.85 (logistic, calibrated on synthetic data). Nothing converts an abstention into a trace.
9. Validation is a seeded synthetic generator (golfer, club arc, physics ball, skies, distractors, shake, slow motion, x264/x265) with tune and held-out splits. It runs in Node and through real headless Chrome.
10. Estimated runtime for a 10 s 4K60 clip on this laptop (M4 Pro, Chrome 155) is about 9 s, roughly 0.9x clip duration, from measured components.

**Key idea 1: impact-anchored, two-body launch test (fixes "tracks the club head").** The club and the ball both pass through the address point at impact, so origin and timing cannot separate them (E2: club passes the origin-time test 73 to 100% of the time). What separates them, independent of scale and fps:
- The ball's early path is a 3D straight line, and perspective maps it to a 2D straight line through the address.
- The ball is always the leading emitter. Smash factor puts it at least 1.14x (down-the-line) and 1.73x (face-on) farther from the address than the club head at the first post-impact frame, with 0 of 2311 violations (E2b).
- The club head's apparent extent is about 2.2x the ball's diameter (E2b).

At 99% ball acceptance, straightness plus width passes 0% of club-head tracks with 2 or more post-impact samples in 29 of 30 view × capture-rate × sample-count strata, and 1% in the worst (face-on 240 fps, 2 samples) (E2c).

**Key idea 2: physics-gated native tracking and completion (fixes "does not track the ball completely").** One fitted 3D flight model does three jobs:
- It places small native-resolution ROIs where multi-frame TBD recovers 1 to 3 px balls. TBD roughly halves the detectable contrast through real x264 (E3).
- It rejects identity steals (airplanes, birds, specks) as model outliers.
- It completes the arc. Down-the-line completion shape error is 0.3 to 0.4% of the frame diagonal (median) when observed until the ball shrinks below 0.5 px, and 2.5 to 3.4% when only 15% of that window is observed. Image-space rational and parabola models reach end errors far above 100% (E4).

## 2. End-to-end architecture and data flow

### 2.1 Passes

```
main thread                         worker (module)
───────────                         ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File ──{analyze,file}──────────────▶ P0 probe: demuxVideo(moov) → frame PTS list, fps, VFR flag,
                                        rotation, codec; demuxAudio (optional) → AudioDecoder → onsets
                                     decode path: WebCodecs VideoDecoder + VideoFrame.copyTo(Y plane)
         ◀─{frame-request}────────── (fallback only: RemoteFrameSource ↔ main <video> frame server)
         ──{frame-data}────────────▶
                                     PA coarse scan: every frame decoded; every m_A-th frame copied
                                        and analyzed, m_A = max(1, round(fps/30)) (≈ 30 Hz):
                                        Y pooled to long edge 960 (display orientation)
                                        → camera similarity per analyzed frame (stabilized coords)
                                        → candidates: 2-3-scale box-DoG, tile-normalized z, tile caps
                                        → spot tracker (stationary ball-like blobs) → vacate events
                                        → foreground energy maps (1/16 res), horizon line (optional)
                                     LA impact & launch scoring (no decode): ImpactHypothesis[≤4]
                                        each with ranked emergence chains + club/ball features
                                     PB native pass from (earliest vacate − 0.25 s) to end:
                                        B1 launch confirmation: address ROI (vacate frame, d0),
                                           emergence ROIs, ray-transform rescue → identity accept/reject
                                        B2 flight tracking per accepted launch: KF → ROI tube → TBD →
                                           observations; periodic warm M3D refit; termination
                                     FC final robust M3D fit (multi-start), residual spline,
                                        completion with σ(t), end reason, per-point status
                                     DC decide: rules R1-R5, confidence, reason codes → result
         ◀─{progress}... {result}───
render-trace: curve from result.points/trajectory, progressive reveal, camera transform, export.
```

### 2.2 Worker protocol (replaces init / frame×N / finish lockstep)

| Direction | Message | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| main → worker | `{type:'analyze', file: File, options:{decode:'auto'\|'codec'\|'element', diagnostics:bool, audio:bool, hint?:{address?:{x,y,t}}}}` | The File is structured-cloned; the worker reads it with `slice()` per sample (never whole). |
| main → worker | `{type:'cancel'}` | Worker aborts and closes the decoder; main also terminates after 2 s. |
| worker → main | `{type:'progress', phase:'probe'\|'scan'\|'launch'\|'track'\|'fit', done, total, message}` | Throttled to 10 Hz. |
| worker → main | `{type:'frame-request', reqId, index, t, coarse?:{w,h}, rois?:[{x,y,w,h,scale}]}` | Element path only. |
| main → worker | `{type:'frame-data', reqId, index, mediaTime, coarse?:{w,h,buffer}, rois?:[{x,y,w,h,buffer}]}` | Buffers transferred. Luma only (main converts RGBA → Y). |
| worker → main | `{type:'result', result}` / `{type:'error', code, message}` | `result` is the contract in sec. 5.6. |

The old protocol stays only in `worker-legacy.js` for `?engine=legacy` comparisons. It has no fallback role and is labeled unverified in the UI.

### 2.3 Memory budget (never all frames)

| Item | Size |
|---|---|
| Demux sample table | ~40 B/sample (7,200 samples = 0.3 MB) |
| One NV12 copy buffer (reused) | 1.5·W·H: 3.1 MB (1080p), 12.4 MB (4K) |
| Coarse luma, current + previous | 2 × 0.5 MB |
| Candidate table (typed arrays, ≤ 48 per analyzed frame) | ≤ 2 MB for 1,800 analyzed frames |
| Foreground energy maps (1/16 res, uint8) | ≤ 15 MB |
| PB ROI ring buffers (≤ 4 hypotheses × ≤ 31 crops × ≤ 192²; 2J + 2Δ + 1 crops) | ≤ 4.6 MB |
| Audio PCM (mono 16 kHz, 30 s, float32) | 1.9 MB |
| **Worker total** | **≈ 25-40 MB** + V8 overhead |

Decoder output frames are closed immediately, with at most 4-6 in flight. The main thread holds no frames (the fallback holds one RGBA frame transiently). This fixes the playback-cache bug (v2-frames-I4) because no cache exists.

### 2.4 Time budget

Measured in headless Chrome 155 on an M4 Pro (E1, E5):
- Decode + full-frame NV12 `copyTo` + JS luma pooling: **2.5 ms/frame** at 1080p30 H.264, **3.6 ms** at 4K60 H.264 High. Decode alone: 837 / 558 fps (1.2 / 1.8 ms); HEVC 1080p 1,773 fps (throughput implies hardware decode).
- Decode + two 256² ROI `copyTo`: **2.2 ms/frame** at 4K60, 1.5 ms at 1080p60.
- Canvas `drawImage` + `getImageData` instead: 17.9 ms (1080p) and 55 ms (4K) per frame, so `copyTo` is mandatory.
- Coarse analysis prototype at 540×960: blob detection 6.5-8.7 ms after optimization (12.7 ms naive); camera motion about 5 ms naive, target 2 ms.
- M3D fit: 60-500 ms final fit in Node; 20-40 ms warm refit.

**Estimate for a 10 s 4K60 clip (600 frames):**
- PA: decode only for the 300 skipped frames, 300 × 1.8 ms = 0.5 s; decode + copy + pool + analysis for the 300 analyzed frames (m_A = 2), 300 × (3.6 + 11 ms) = 4.4 s. Subtotal ≈ 4.9 s.
- PB: about 420 frames × (2.2 + 2.0 ms) = 1.8 s.
- Launch scoring + fits: about 1 s.

Total ≈ **8-9 s (about 0.9x clip duration)**.
- If every frame is analyzed with the unoptimized 18 ms analysis: ≈ 16 s (1.6x).
- A 10 s 240 fps 1080p clip (2,400 frames, m_A = 8): PA ≈ 2,400 × 1.2 ms + 300 × 13.5 ms ≈ 7 s; PB ≈ 4 s (stride adapts near apex); total ≈ 12 s.
- Phones: assumed 2.5-4x slower (not measured). A "fast" profile (coarse long edge 720, m_A for 20 Hz) targets about 20-25 s for 10 s clips.

Element-path fallback (seek + rVFC + draw + readback), measured: 30 ms/frame (1080p30), 44 ms (1080p60), 117 ms (4K60). A 1080p30 10 s clip takes ≈ 16 s, but 4K60 ≈ 70 s. The UI warns when this path is used.

## 3. Launch, impact, and club-head rejection

### 3.1 Units (all gates are scale- and fps-invariant)

- `d0`: the address-ball diameter in native px. PA estimates it from the DoG scale and second moments; B1 refines it with a disc+Gaussian fit.
- `d(t)`: the expected diameter, `d0·Z0/Z(t)` from the model or measured. `d̄` is the mean expected diameter over a chain.
- Frame index `k` is the presentation index from the container. `F = 1/fps_file`. Motion gates are **per frame**: per-frame displacement depends on the capture rate, which is identical for 240 fps real time and 240→30 slow motion.
- Durations use `max(N frames, T seconds)`. Physics uses real time `t_r = t_video/τ`.
- Image directions are measured with y up.

### 3.2 Address spots (PA)

1. Candidates come from box-DoG scales with inner/outer radii (1,4) and (2,7) coarse px, plus (4,12) when the clip's largest stationary blobs exceed 12 coarse px. Detection is polarity-agnostic, `z = |DoG|/σ_tile ≥ 5`, where `σ_tile` is the MAD of DoG in a 60×60 tile. At most 6 candidates per tile, so a bright shirt cannot take all slots (v2-roi-I2).
2. Spot linking: same stabilized position within 0.35·d, luma within ±15%, same polarity, gaps ≤ 2 analyzed frames.
3. A spot is ball-like if all of these hold:
   - second-moment eigenvalue ratio ≤ 1.8 and fill ≥ 0.55 (excludes standing tees and thin reflections);
   - positive polarity, or |contrast| ≥ 6σ for balls on white mats;
   - saturation ≤ 0.6 (yellow balls allowed);
   - coarse diameter in [2.5 px, 0.08 × short side].
4. Soft position prior: `y ≥ 0.3·H` scores higher. It is not a gate.
5. Eligible when present in ≥ 80% of frames over ≥ `max(8 frames, 0.25 s)`.

### 3.3 Vacate event and impact time

For each eligible spot, let `k_last` be its last present frame.

1. **Persistence.** Absence ratio ≥ 0.9 over `[k_last+3, k_last+3+Q]`, `Q = max(8 frames, 0.3 s)`, clipped at clip end. Frames `k_last+1` and `k_last+2` are excluded because the club and ball can still overlap the address (E6: the first post-impact frame is 0.1-1.8 d0 from the address at p05).
2. **Change.** The normalized cross-correlation (NCC) of a 2·d0 box between the pre-vacate mean `[k_last−6, k_last−1]` and the post mean `[k_last+4, k_last+10]` is ≤ 0.6. The post patch is stable (frame-to-frame NCC ≥ 0.8): the spot became tee, grass, or mat permanently.
3. **Impulse, not ramp (F2, F33).** Let `s_j` be the departing object's distance from the address at frame `k_last+j` (j ≥ 1) and `σ_j = s_{j+1} − s_j`.
   - A launch chain must satisfy `σ_1 ≥ max(0.5·d0, 0.6·σ_2)` and `σ_{j+1} ≤ 1.25·σ_j + 0.3·d0`: full speed within one frame, then constant (face-on) or perspective-decaying (down-the-line).
   - **Gradual-departure veto:** if any candidate track that starts within 0.5·d0 of the address in `[k_last, k_last+3]` has `σ_1 < 0.5·d0` and growing steps, the vacate is vetoed. Takeaway glints, gloves, feet, and raked balls start below 0.3·d0/frame and ramp for many frames.
   - E6: the ball's first full step is ≥ 2.1·d0/frame (p05) at every capture rate up to 240 fps. The 0.5·d0 floor leaves margin for 960 fps bursts.
4. **Club passage (soft).** Foreground energy in the annulus `[0.6·d0, 8·d0]` around the address peaks at `k_peak ∈ [k_last−3, k_last+2]`. Score = `log(E_peak/E_baseline)`, baseline = median over the previous 1 s.
5. **Audio (soft, optional).** Decode the demuxed AAC track with WebCodecs `AudioDecoder`, or `decodeAudioData` for files ≤ 64 MB. Onset function: positive log-energy derivative of high-passed (> 2 kHz) 2 ms frames, with peaks ≥ 8 dB above the running median. An onset within `±(1.5F + 30 ms)` of `t(k_last)+F/2` adds ≤ 1 nat to the impact log-score. Audio can never create a hypothesis and its absence never penalizes. Ranges are full of other golfers' clicks, so it is a tie-breaker only. It is ignored when τ̂ > 1.5 (slow motion) or if the file has no audio.
6. Rank hypotheses by vacate quality, not size. Keep ≤ 4.

### 3.4 Emergence chains and the ball-vs-club test

**Candidates.** Moving candidates in frames `k_last+1 … k_last+K_e`, where `K_e = min(12, frames until s > 0.4·diag)`.
- Search radius `s ≤ (k − k_last + 1)·70·d0` (E6: face-on 30 fps p95 is 58 d0/frame).
- Exclude points within 0.6·d0 of the address from `k_last+3` on.
- PB re-runs this at native resolution, full frame rate, in an ROI scaled so `d0 ≈ 8 px` (max scale 1).

**Ray transform (faint or blurred launches, F28).** Resample the registered residual `r_k = I_k − ½(I_{k−Δ}+I_{k+Δ})` (post-impact frames only) into polar coordinates around the address: θ in 0.5° steps over [-25°, 205°] (y up), s in d0/2 steps, integrating a 1·d0-wide strip across the ray.
- A ball streak lies along a ray; its strip integral grows with its length (flux is conserved under blur).
- A dynamic-programming pass per θ finds the best monotone `s_k` path obeying the step rules in item 3 of sec. 3.3.
- Cost ≈ 2M samples per hypothesis, about 10 ms.

**Chains.** Exhaustive search with pruning, since there are ≤ 10 candidates per frame near the address. Each chain `(c_1..c_m)` has strictly increasing `s`, steps ≥ 0.5·d0/frame (after the first), each new point inside a wedge of ±max(3°, atan(0.6·d0/s)) of the current line through the address, and gaps ≤ 2 frames. Keep the top 8.

**Features**, computed at native resolution in B1:

| Feature | Definition | Ball (E2) | Club (E2) | Hard gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| f1 straightness | RMS ⟂ residual to best line through address, in d̄ | p99 0.16-0.23 | median 0.2-4.2 | ≤ 0.35 when m ≥ 3 |
| f2 projective profile | RMS residual of `s(t)=α(t−t0)/(1+β(t−t0))`, β ≥ −0.05/frame, in d0 | p99 0.04-0.30 | median 0.0-0.6 | ≤ 0.5 when m ≥ 3 |
| f3 origin time | t0 ∈ [k_last−0.5, k_last+1.5] frames | passes | passes (73-100%) | gate (kills F2 sources, not clubs) |
| f4 lead | farthest moving object from the address within ±15° at each frame | always (0/2311 violations) | never leads | soft |
| f5 width | ⟂ width ≤ 1.35·d_exp + 1 px, `d_exp = d0/(1+β(t−t0))` (same β as f2) | ratio p99 1.0-1.6 | median 1.9-2.9 | gate |
| f6 flux | ∫(luma − bg) / address flux ∈ [0.3, 1.6], scaled by `(d/d0)²` | — | specular glints vary | soft |
| f7 streak axis | if length/width ≥ 2: axis within max(4°, atan(2/len)) of the chain line | ≤ 0.3° | 1.5-5.9° median | soft |
| f8 club passage, audio | from sec. 3.3 | — | — | soft |

The combined straightness + width gate passes **0%** of club tracks at m ≥ 2 in 29 of 30 view × fps × m strata, and 1% in face-on 240 fps m=2, at 99% ball acceptance (E2c, 15% width-measurement noise). Straightness alone passes 0-7% at m = 6 (E2, E2c). The E2 club model has no ball-sized specular glints, so the generator must include them (sec. 9.1) before these rates are trusted.

**Ball log-likelihood ratio.** `LLR = Σ log p_ball(f_i)/p_club(f_i)` over the soft features, using class-conditional histograms learned on the tune split (Laplace-smoothed). Each term is clamped to ±3 nats so no single learned feature dominates. Hard gates f1, f2, f3, and f5 are applied first and do not depend on learned densities.

**Accepting a ball identity for an impact hypothesis:**
1. Best chain passes the hard gates, `LLR ≥ λ_acc` (tuned for club acceptance ≤ 0.5% on tune), and `LLR(best) − LLR(best chain of a different object) ≥ μ` (else `identity_conflict`).
2. Sample count:
   - `m ≥ 4` if the chain stays in frame for 4 frames;
   - otherwise (frame exit) `m ≥ 2`, with f4, f5, and f7 passing;
   - `m = 1` (face-on 30 fps: the ball is in frame 2+ times in only 28% of cases, E2) is off by default and enabled only if holdout club acceptance for single-sample launches is ≤ 1%. Single-sample launches yield `partial` with `single_sample_launch`. When disabled, report `ball_left_frame_too_fast`.
3. Identity must then be **confirmed by flight** (sec. 6, R2) before any completion.

### 3.5 View, slow motion, practice swings, multiple balls

- **Down-the-line vs face-on.** No view classifier is used at launch; every feature is view-agnostic. The view is inferred afterward from the fitted azimuth β (|β| < 45° is down-the-line-like) and only selects the landing and fade policy.
- **Slow motion.** Every launch gate is per frame and in d0, so 240→30 slow motion and 240 fps real time behave identically. Duration windows use `max(N frames, T s)`. τ affects only physics: its prior comes from the step size after the first moving frame (E6 medians, d0/frame: down-the-line 11.8 / 8.3 / 5.0 / 2.8 at 30/60/120/240 fps capture; face-on 48.8 / 24.9 / 12.5 / 6.3). Capture-fps metadata, when present, overrides it.
- **Practice swing or whiff.** No spot passes the vacate test, so there is no hypothesis: `no_reliable_track` + `no_launch` ("No ball left the tee in this clip"). A clipped tee fails the round-shape gate, the impulse/leading tests, or flight plausibility (sec. 6).
- **Multiple stationary balls.** Only vacating spots get hypotheses. Neighbors stay put and are excluded as static. Two vacates are tracked independently, so neither can evict the other (fixes F19). The best confirmed flight wins and the other is reported with `multiple_shots`.

## 4. Flight tracking of a faint or small ball (B2)

### 4.1 Prediction and ROI tube

- **Filter.** A Kalman filter (KF) per accepted launch in stabilized native px, state `(x, y, vx, vy, ax, ay)` per frame. Process noise scales with `d(t)` and `|a|`. For the first 6 inlier observations the KF extrapolates the launch chain's projective profile. After that, its control input is the acceleration predicted by the current M3D fit, refit warm every 10 observations or after two innovations with χ² > 9.
- **ROI half-size.** `h_k = clamp(3·√λmax(Σ_k) + 1.5·d_k + J·|v_k|·m + 4, 12, 192)` px. Processing scale `s_k = min(1, 6/d_k)` keeps kernels small for big early balls.
- **Crops.** Pulled from the decoded frame with `VideoFrame.copyTo({rect})` (even-aligned, measured 2.2 ms/frame for two 256² ROIs at 4K), in coded coordinates mapped from display coordinates.
- **Registration.** Crops are mapped by the frame's camera similarity, then refined locally (±1.5 px SAD with parabolic sub-pixel fit, ball core masked). This removes cloud-edge and horizon residuals left by global motion error.

### 4.2 Detection (roi-detect)

1. **Residual.** `r_k = I_k − ½(I_{k−Δ}+I_{k+Δ})` with `Δ = clamp(⌈2.5·d_k/|v_k|⌉, 1, 12)` stride units, so static specks, poles, and nets cancel. Per-ROI gain match handles auto-exposure.
2. **Matched filter.** Gaussian with σ = max(0.7, 0.35·d_k) px. Noise σ is the MAD over the ROI excluding the predicted core, giving a z map. Both polarities count (a ball can be darker than a bright overcast sky).
3. **TBD.** `S(x,v) = Σ_{j=−J..J} z_{k+j}(x + v·j)/√(2J+1)`, J = 3 (J = 2 when |v| > 6 px/frame). Velocities are a 3×3 grid (5×5 when σ_v is large) around `v_pred`, spacing 0.4·m px/frame. Detection at frame k is delayed by J + Δ frames, using a ring buffer of 2J + 2Δ + 1 registered crops.
4. **Threshold, empirical null.** Each frame, the same statistic is computed on two decoy ROIs offset perpendicular to the path by 4·h_k. `T_k = max(T_min(N_tests), q_0.995(null history))`.
   - E3 at 6 Mbps-equivalent quality: 1% per-frame false alarm at 4.23σ (single) and 4.92σ (TBD, 15² × 9).
   - At 2 Mbps the same false-alarm rate needs 6.09σ / 8.58σ, so fixed thresholds would either flood or starve.
5. **Accept** when `S ≥ T_k`, the single-frame z at the peak is ≥ 2.0, polarity is consistent (a flip is allowed when ROI background mean changes > 25 levels), and size ≤ 3·max(d_k, 1.5 px). Sub-pixel position comes from a quadratic peak fit on the stacked map. Covariance comes from peak curvature and SNR.

E3 detection rates (hit within 1.5 px) through real x264 at 960×720, 6 Mbps:

| Diameter | Contrast | Single-frame | TBD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 px | +12 | 43% | 100% |
| 3 px | +8 | 31% | 100% |
| 3 px | +6 | 6% | 80% |
| 1.3 px | +20 | 31% | 100% |
| 2 px | −8 (dark) | 0% | 46% |
| 2 px | −16 (dark) | 74% | 100% |

v2's bright-mask refiner rule finds 0% of dark balls and needs contrast ≥ +20 at ≥ 2 px.

### 4.3 Camera motion, sky vs ground

- **PA camera motion.** SAD search ±6 on a 1/4-resolution pyramid level, then 48 textured patches (minimum structure-tensor eigenvalue above a floor) with ±3 px search and parabolic sub-pixel refinement. An IRLS similarity fit (Tukey) excludes golfer patches as outliers. Integrated into stabilized coordinates; `cameraSim` is stored per frame.
- If the inlier ratio stays < 0.5 for > 20% of the flight window (textureless sky pans), the result abstains with `camera_motion_uncompensated`.
- **Lens-fixed specks.** Blobs that stay fixed in raw image coordinates while the camera moves are masked.
- **Sky vs ground.** There are no hard-coded sky regions (v2 used `y < 0.55h`). Local noise estimation, polarity freedom, and two-sided backgrounds adapt automatically. Over textured ground the expected-size gate tightens to 2·max(d, 1.5).

### 4.4 Occlusion, frame exit, slow motion, termination

- **Occlusion.** PA foreground energy (golfer) and static high-contrast vertical structures (poles) form masks. Misses inside masks do not count; the KF coasts.
- **Lost and re-acquisition.** Lost after `max(8 frames, 0.25 s real)` unmasked misses (stride units). Search continues with a growing ROI until `max(0.6 s real, 0.25 × elapsed flight)`. Re-acquisition needs **3 of 5** consistent detections, so single false alarms cannot extend a trace (F17).
- **Frame exit.** When the prediction ±2σ leaves the frame, searching stops. If M3D predicts re-entry (a high wedge from down-the-line), search resumes at the predicted re-entry frame. End reason `frame_exit`.
- **Slow motion and apex.** Stride `m = clamp(⌊1.0 px / |v_k|⌋, 1, 8)`: when image speed is tiny, only every m-th frame is copied and analyzed (decode still covers all frames). The first 0.1 s real time always uses m = 1.
- **Termination.** Model landing time + 0.3 s passed, or lost and re-acquisition expired, or exit without re-entry, or clip end.

### 4.5 Identity-steal prevention (airplane, bird, speck, neighbor ball)

1. Gating by the physics-informed KF (χ² ≤ 13.8, 2 dof).
2. The TBD velocity grid around the predicted velocity: a constant-velocity airplane accumulates only while velocities match.
3. Appearance trend: size ≤ expected, flux non-increasing within noise, polarity consistency.
4. **MHT.** When two detections pass in one frame, keep up to 4 branches and resolve after ≥ 5 frames by fit residual.
5. **Known distractors.** Long, straight, slow sky tracks found in PA are masked; detections within 1.5·d of one are flagged `ambiguous` and excluded from the fit unless their TBD velocity differs from the distractor's by > 3σ.
6. **Robust final fit.** Observations with residual > 4σ become `rejected`. A trailing run of rejects truncates the observed segment, so a plane cannot "extend" the flight (v2-global-I2).
7. There is no length bonus anywhere. Length helps only by tightening the fit covariance.

## 5. Trajectory model, smoothing, and full-arc completion

### 5.1 Model M3D (flight-model.js)

**Camera.** Pinhole with the principal point at the center.
- Focal f: prior log-normal around `f_nom = (long_side/2)/tan(34.5°)` (phone main camera, about 69° long-axis FOV), σ_log 0.12. A 0.5·f_nom mixture component handles the ultra-wide lens, and adds one radial distortion term k1 (prior N(−0.08, 0.05)). Metadata focal length overrides when present.
- Pitch θ: prior N(8°, 12°). Roll φ: prior N(0, 3°).
- When PA finds a horizon line (dominant near-horizontal edge between a smooth bright region above and texture below), the priors tighten to σ_θ = 2° and σ_φ = 1°. This addresses E4's worst cases, which ended about 100 px above the horizon because pitch was weakly constrained.

**Scale.** Address depth `Z0 = f·D/d0` (D = 42.67 mm). The ball's own size anchors the scene scale.

**Dynamics** (world frame, gravity −Y):

```
dv/dt = g − k_D·|v|·v + k_L·|v|²·n_L(ψ)
k_D = ρ·A·C_D/(2m),  k_L = ρ·A·C_L/(2m)
```

- `n_L = unit(ω̂ × v̂)`, where ω̂ is the horizontal backspin axis rotated by spin-axis tilt ψ about v̂. ψ produces draws, fades, hooks, and slices symmetrically.
- C_D and C_L are constant, with priors N(0.25, 0.04) and N(0.22, 0.08). There is no spin decay and no wind; the generator has both, so the mismatch is deliberate.

**Launch.** From the 3D address point with speed s0 (prior logN(52 m/s, 0.35)), elevation α (prior N(18°, 11°)), and azimuth β (vague, initialized from the image launch direction).

**Time scale.** `t_video = τ·t_real`. Prior from capture-fps metadata, otherwise a mixture over {1, 2, 4, 8} weighted by the launch-step likelihood (E6), with log τ refined continuously (σ 0.5).

**Integration.** RK4 at 1/240 s real time (1/480 s for the first 0.1 s). Landing is when height relative to the address drops below 0 after the apex.

### 5.2 Robust fitting

- **Data.** Observations `(t_k, x_k, y_k, Σ_k)`, plus early diameter observations `d_k` (σ_d = 0.15·d) that pin the depth rate.
- **Initialization.** Grid over α ∈ {6°, 12°, 20°, 30°, 40°}, β = image direction + {0°, ±20°}, s0 ∈ {35, 50, 65} m/s, and the τ modes. The 3 best by MAP cost go to Levenberg-Marquardt (Huber δ = 2.5σ, ≤ 40 iterations). The best MAP solution wins.
- **Outliers.** Residuals > 4σ become `rejected`, then one refit. If inliers < 70%, a RANSAC re-initialization fits the first 4 + 4 random observations (rare).
- **Uncertainty.** Laplace covariance `(JᵀWJ + H_prior)⁻¹`, propagated to predicted positions σ(t) through the projection Jacobian.
- **Measured (E4, 30 shots per view per frame rate, 3% outliers).** Down-the-line, observing a fraction of the visible window (in frame and ≥ 0.5 px):

| Fraction observed | Shape error, median | Shape error, p90 |
|---|---|---|
| 15% | 2.5-3.4% | 5.2-6.9% |
| 35% | 0.9-1.7% | 3.7-5.5% |
| 60% | 0.7-0.9% | 5.9-6.6% |
| 100% | 0.3-0.4% | 1.7-3.5% |

  Errors are max shape error over the predicted segment, % of frame diagonal. The p90 that fails to improve from 35% to 60% observed comes from local minima and pitch/horizon errors (E4 diagnostic: worst fits end about 100 px above the true landing near the horizon); multi-start plus the horizon prior target this. Landing-time error is median 3-7% and p90 9-14% of flight time. Face-on in-frame completion error is median 0.1-1.5%. Rational image models have median shape errors of 4-22% and parabolas 9-11%, both with end errors far above 100%, so they are unusable for completion.
- **With τ unknown (E4b).** Slow motion 8x: τ̂ median 8.2 (p10 7.3, p90 9.3); shape error median 2.5%, p90 8.0% at 35% observed.

### 5.3 Smoothing without dropping observations

The drawn curve is `C(t) = M3D(t) + ρ(t)`.
- ρ is a cubic smoothing spline of inlier residuals (smoothing by generalized cross-validation, |ρ| ≤ 1.5·d̄) over the observed span.
- Beyond the last observation, ρ decays to 0 with a 0.15 s real-time constant, so there is no kink.
- Interior observations are never dropped. Outliers are explicitly `rejected` and listed in `diagnostics.observations`.
- Manual points get σ = 0.5 px and become hard spline constraints: the curve passes within 1 px of each click (fixes ui-render-export-I1).

### 5.4 Landing, fade, exit per view

| Case | Rule | End reason |
|---|---|---|
| Down-the-line-like (\|β\| < 45°) | Continue until model height returns to 0. Alpha fades over the last 0.25 s real time of the descent; a short landing fade when the landing point is visible and near. | `landing` |
| Face-on-like | Continue off-frame; the canvas clips it. A landing inside the frame (short wedge, wide frame) is honored. | `frame_exit` / `landing` |
| Uncertainty | When the 1σ major axis of σ(t) exceeds 3% of diagonal, alpha fades linearly to 0 at 6%. If that happens before 50% of the predicted remainder, status becomes `partial` + `completion_uncertain`. | `fade` |
| Clip ends first (always for slow motion) | Preview: the final frame shows the full arc. Export: optional freeze tail of min(2.0 s, remainder), with the remaining arc animated time-compressed, then held. | arc: `landing`/`fade` as above; observed segment: `observedEnd = 'clip_end'` |

`trajectory.endReason` says why the drawn arc ends. `trajectory.observedEnd ∈ {'lost', 'frame_exit', 'occluded', 'clip_end', 'landing'}` says why observation stopped. The two are reported separately so the benchmark and UI can tell "ball faded, arc predicted to landing" from "ball watched all the way down".

### 5.5 Predicted points and visual treatment

- `points[]` holds one entry per source frame time from impact to the end of the arc, continuing past the clip end at the same interval. Each entry is `{t, x, y, status, sigma}`:
  - `launch`: the address point at t0;
  - `observed`: an inlier detection at that frame; the drawn position is C(t), and the raw position is kept in diagnostics;
  - `interpolated`: between observations (miss or occlusion);
  - `predicted`: after the last observation, or before re-acquisition.
- There are no pre-impact points, which fixes v2's 0.2 s early start (v2-frames-I8).
- Rendering is one continuous TV-style arc: the same stroke for observed and predicted, the head dot at the current model position, width scaled as today, glow optional. The line grows with video time and starts at t0.
- `?debug=trace` draws observed points as dots, predicted segments dashed, rejected points as red ×, and the σ envelope.

## 6. Abstention and confidence

**Rules. All must hold for `tracked`.**
- **R1 Launch confirmed.** Valid vacate (sec. 3.3) and accepted emergence (sec. 3.4).
- **R2 Identity confirmed by flight.** The M3D fit over the first observations has inliers ≥ 80%, normalized RMS ≤ 1.5, and prior z-scores ≤ 3 (rejects tee flights and club tracks that stop). Plus either ≥ 8 observations spanning ≥ 0.15 s real time (down-the-line-like) or a predicted frame exit within 2 frames of the last observation (face-on-like). **Arc completion requires R1 and R2.**
- **R3 No conflict.** The best hypothesis beats the next hypothesis (different impact or different emitter) by confidence margin ≥ 0.15, and the ball/club LLR margin is ≥ μ.
- **R4 Camera usable.** Motion inliers ≥ 0.5 over the flight window.
- **R5 Calibrated confidence.** p ≥ 0.85.

**Statuses.**
- `tracked`: R1-R5 hold and completion reached `landing`, `frame_exit`, or `fade` normally.
- `partial`, either of:
  - (a) R1-R4 hold, p ≥ 0.6, completion truncated: `completion_uncertain`;
  - (b) R1 holds but R2 does not (short observed track, not a frame exit): **observed-only trace, no completion**, `tracking_ended_early`.
  - Partial results are exportable after review. Bulk mode skips them.
- `no_reliable_track` with `points: []` and one or more of: `no_launch`, `ball_not_found_after_impact`, `identity_conflict`, `ball_left_frame_too_fast`, `camera_motion_uncompensated`, `flight_inconsistent`, `low_confidence`, `decoder_limited`, `no_frames`.

**Confidence.** `p = σ(w·φ + b)` by logistic regression on the tune split. Label: identity correct and completion shape error ≤ 5% of diagonal.
- Features φ: LLR margin, vacate score, club-passage score, audio alignment, log #observations, real-time span, fit normalized RMS, inlier ratio, max prior z, end σ, camera inlier ratio, competing-hypothesis margin.
- The UI shows "Confidence (synthetic-calibrated) 92%". Reliability diagram and expected calibration error (ECE) are reported per benchmark run.

**What replaces the broken fallback.** Nothing automatic.
1. The legacy-link fallback (tracker.js `resultGlobal`, d63ee5a) is deleted in U0.
2. Invariants are enforced by tests:
   - status `no_reliable_track` implies `points.length === 0`;
   - `normalizeTrackReport` may only downgrade a status, never upgrade it;
   - a no-ball practice swing is never exportable.
3. The abstention UI states the reason and offers:
   - **(a) "Help find the ball":** one tap on the address ball (optionally a second on the ball in flight) re-runs v3 with the spot given. Only spot discovery is skipped; R1-R5 still apply.
   - **(b) Manual adjust**, with the CSS fixed. Manual traces stay labeled `manual` (fixes v2-ui-export-I6), and ≥ 3 manual points can be completed with M3D (`predicted`).

## 7. Frame timing and invariant gates

**Frame times.**
- **Container (primary).** `stts` + `ctts` + `elst` give exact per-frame PTS in presentation order: `fps_nominal = 1/median(ΔPTS)`, VFR flag when the p95/p5 ratio of Δ is > 1.1. Capture-fps hints come from metadata when present (Android `com.android.capture.fps`), else τ is fitted.
- **WebCodecs path.** Every frame is decoded once and timestamps equal container PTS. E1: 180/180 (1080p30), 360/360 (1080p60), 300/300 (4K60), 720/720 (240 fps), and 180/180 (HEVC) frames, 0 duplicates, 0 gaps, monotone, matching the frame-index barcode.
- **Element fallback.**
  - Targets are frame centers: `(pts_i + pts_{i+1})/2` when demuxable, else `t0 + (i+0.5)/fps` with fps from about 20 rVFC callbacks during muted playback.
  - After each seek, wait for `seeked` and rVFC (≤ 250 ms). Use `metadata.mediaTime` as t. Dedupe equal mediaTime (re-seek once to the next center).
  - E1: frame-center seeks give **0 duplicates and 0 skips** at 1080p30, 1080p60, 4K60, and 240 fps, with rVFC mediaTime exact. The current i/fps grid gives 58 duplicates + 57 skips of 180 (30 fps) and 118 + 117 of 360 (60 fps).
- **Node runner.** `ffmpeg -fps_mode passthrough` plus per-frame `pts_time`, never `fps=30`. It then sees the browser's exact frame list, fixing v2-eval-I1 (BALL-03 parity).
- **Rotation.** The demuxer reads the `tkhd` matrix (E1 read 270 for an ffmpeg `-display_rotation 90` file). The sign convention is pinned by a test against the `<video>` element's rendering. Analysis runs in display orientation.

## 9. Synthetic benchmark

### 9.1 Generator design (tests/synth)

**World.** Meters, Y up, Z toward the target, ball at the origin.
- **Ground:** procedural grass (multi-octave value noise, mowing stripes, perspective-correct by ray-plane intersection), optional hitting mat with lines, divots.
- **Background:** tree line with a noisy top edge, range poles, nets (mesh with transparency, sometimes in front of the camera), flags (waving), yardage boards, distant static balls.
- **Sky:** blue gradient, bright overcast (ball near sky luma), haze, sunset (dark), with drifting value-noise clouds and sun glare.

**Golfer** (articulated capsules, rendered with a z-buffer):
- Pelvis/torso rotation about a tilted spine, arms from shoulders to the grip, head and cap, legs.
- Clothing palettes include white shirt, glove, belt, and shoes (F2).
- Swing kinematics: hands on a circle of radius 0.6-0.75 m around the lead shoulder in a plane tilted 44-58°; wrist hinge releases from about 90° at the top to 0° at impact.
- Tempo: backswing 0.75-1.0 s, downswing 0.25-0.3 s real time; waggle and forward press; right- and left-handed.
- The club head passes through the ball at t0 with speed `ball_speed/smash` (smash 1.35-1.5).

**Club.** Shaft (thin, gray or black, specular streak) plus head: driver (black crown, silver face edge) or iron/wedge (chrome with sun-angle specular glints, **including ball-sized glints**, an adversarial case for f5).

**Ball.**
- 42.67 mm Lambertian + specular sphere: white, or yellow in 5% of clips.
- On a tee (stays in 70% of tee clips, flies in 30%) or on grass/mat.
- Shadow at address.

**Ball physics (independent of the tracker's M3D).**
- Spin-dependent `C_D = 0.21 + 0.22·S`, `C_L = min(0.34, 0.10 + 1.15·S)`, where S is the spin factor.
- Spin decay τ_s ≈ 25 s, gusting wind, slight terrain height variation at landing.
- Shots: driver, 7-iron, wedge, topped/ground balls (2%), with launch, spin, and axis tilt randomized.

**Distractors.**
- Airplane (constant angular velocity, sometimes a blinking light), birds (dark, flapping size oscillation, erratic), insects near the lens (fast blur).
- Lens dust specks fixed in image coordinates, people walking.
- A **neighbor bay's ball flight** (a real ballistic object, F8/F31), and other balls on the mat or pile.

**Camera.**
- Pinhole with phone FOVs (main about 69° long axis; ultra-wide 110° in 10% of clips; mild barrel distortion).
- Placements: down-the-line behind the ball, behind the hands, or offset; face-on at 3-8 m; oblique 30-60°.
- Pitch -2 to 20°, roll ±3°, portrait or landscape.
- Handheld shake (an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process plus 2-10 Hz tremor, 0.05-0.5°), follow-pan, digital zoom ramp.
- Auto-exposure drift, white balance, vignetting.
- Exposure 1/60-1/4000 s (temporal supersampling of moving layers only), rolling shutter readout 10-33 ms, sensor noise (shot + read, luma-dependent).

**Rendering.**
- The static environment is rendered once per clip per pose; shake and pan warp it by the rotation homography.
- Dynamic layers (golfer, club, ball, distractors) are composited in bounding boxes with sub-frame motion-blur samples.
- Noise comes from a per-clip bank with per-frame offsets.
- Raw frames are piped to ffmpeg with backpressure and a fresh buffer per frame. E7 hit the reuse bug: Node `stream.write` keeps a reference to the buffer.
- Measured prototype: 37-52 ms/frame at 1080×1920 in Node, so a 6 s 60 fps clip takes about 15 s. Parallel workers on 14 cores give about 10 minutes for 600 clips.

**Encoding.**
- libx264 High (phone-like rates: 1080p30 16 Mbps, 1080p60 24, 4K60 50, plus a 6 Mbps-equivalent low-rate variant) and libx265 `hvc1`, in mp4 or mov.
- `-display_rotation` variants.
- Slow motion: render at capture times (240/120 fps), stamp output at 30 fps.
- 240 fps real time; VFR jitter and dropped or duplicated frames via explicit timestamps.

**Audio.** An impact click (broadband transient, A/V offset ±20 ms), neighbor clicks at random times, wind noise, AAC. Absent or time-stretched for slow motion.

### 9.2 Scenario matrix and failure-mode coverage

**Factors.**
- view {dtl-ball, dtl-hands, dtl-offset, fo-rh, fo-lh, oblique}
- capture/playback {30, 60, 120, 240 real time; 120→30; 240→30}
- format {1080×1920 portrait, 2160×3840 portrait, 1920×1080 landscape, rotated-coded}
- club {driver, 7-iron, wedge, topped}
- faintness: sky {blue, overcast-bright, haze, sunset, tree-line backdrop} × camera distance × bitrate, binned by GT expected SNR at 50 m {≥ 6, 3-6, 2-3, < 2}
- confounders (each 0/1, ≤ 3 per clip): practice, whiff, multiball, tee-flies, white-clothes, club glint, neighbor ball, plane, birds, speck, net/poles/flags, shake {low, high}, pan, zoom, auto-exposure, golfer occlusion, frame-exit-top/side, low-bitrate, VFR/duplicates, audio {aligned, misaligned, absent}

**Sampling.** Stratified Latin-hypercube with constraints (for example, no practice swing with neighbor ball). Every failure mode F1-F34 must appear in ≥ 10 tune clips and ≥ 10 holdout clips. A clean baseline exists for each view × capture cell.

**Splits.**
- **Tune:** seeds 1-400, 320 clips, 4-8 s each, impact at 1-2 s.
- **Holdout:** seeds 100001-100300, 240 clips. 30% are **style-shifted**: sky palettes, golfer proportions, club materials, noise and bitrate ranges, and FOVs outside the tune ranges.
- **Long set:** 20 clips of 20-30 s for memory/timing. **Perf set:** 10 clips of 10 s 4K60.
- Holdout files are generated once, checksummed, and evaluated only at milestones, logged with the code hash. Tuning scripts cannot read holdout reports.

### 9.3 Ground truth per clip (`clip.gt.json`)

- `meta`: factors, seed, fps, τ, rotation, intrinsics, per-frame extrinsics, exposure.
- `impact: {t0, frame, address:{x,y}, d0}`.
- `expected: 'trace'|'abstain'|'either'`, with `failureModes: ['F1', ...]`.
- `frames[]: {t, ball:{x,y,diam,inFrame,occluded,expectedSnr}, club:{x,y,size}, distractors[...]}` (display coordinates, exact frame PTS).
- `trajectory3d` sampled at 1 ms until landing; `arc2d` (the full projected path including off-frame); `landing:{t,x,y,inFrame}`.

### 9.4 Metrics

A point matches when `|p − g| ≤ max(2 px, 0.75·d(t))` at the same frame time.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Club-head false-track rate | Clips where ≥ 2 of the first 6 post-impact `observed` points lie within 1.0 club-head size of the GT club and > 2·d from the GT ball |
| Launch error | \|t_launch − t0\| in frames; launch point error in d0 |
| Identity correctness | Matched fraction of `observed` points; identity switch = ≥ 3 consecutive unmatched observed points while GT ball is in frame |
| Observed recall | Matched observed / GT-visible frames (in frame, not occluded, expectedSnr ≥ 2), stratified by SNR bin |
| Position error | Median and p95 of \|p − g\| (px and d) |
| Arc completion | Over `predicted` points: time-aligned and shape (min distance to `arc2d`) error, % diag; end-point error; landing-time error (% of flight time) |
| Abstention correctness | Confusion vs `expected`: false-trace rate (traced on `abstain`), coverage (traced on `trace`), and the safety metric **P(wrong \| traced)** |
| Calibration | Reliability diagram, ECE of `confidence.p` |
| Runtime and memory | Per-phase ms; peak heap and tab RSS (CDP) |
| Parity | Node vs browser: decision, launch frame, median Δposition |

## 10. Risks, limits of synthetic validation, and overfitting control

**What synthetic tests cannot prove:**
- Real club-head appearance: specular glints, carbon crowns, turf spray at impact, real motion-blur and rolling-shutter profiles.
- Phone hardware encoders. Apple HEVC at low light may erase a 1-2 px ball more than x264 does.
- HDR/HLG tone mapping, and real auto-exposure and stabilization behavior. Phone electronic stabilization warps frames non-rigidly.
- Real sky textures (contrails, haze gradients) and range clutter, especially many neighbor balls in the air.
- Slow-motion pipelines that interpolate frames (some Android modes), and edit-list time remapping in iPhone originals.
- User camera placement distributions, and real flights (gusts, elevation changes, mud balls).

**Technical risks and mitigations:**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| WebCodecs coverage (`copyTo` formats, Firefox HEVC, opaque frames) | Format adapters, element fallback, UI warning |
| Demuxer edge cases (fragmented MP4, multiple edit lists) | Detect and route to the element path; fixture corpus in U1 |
| Wrong focal prior (telephoto or zoom), weakly constrained pitch | Horizon prior, metadata focal, uncertainty fade, `completion_uncertain` |
| Face-on at 30 fps is physically limited to one or two samples | Honest abstention; single-sample launches gated by holdout evidence |
| Phone runtime | Fast profile; stride adaptation |
| Learned ball/club densities could encode generator quirks | Hard physics gates (f1, f2, f3, f5) that need no learning; per-feature LLR clamp ±3 nats; ablation report (each cue removed in turn) |

**Limiting overfitting to the generator:**
1. The tracker model and the generator use different aerodynamics, integrators, and camera noise models (E4 already ran with that mismatch).
2. Domain randomization, plus a 30% style-shifted holdout reported separately. A generalization gap larger than 2x the confidence-interval width blocks release.
3. An adversarial scenario set written by a different agent from failure-mode descriptions only: ball-sized club glints, neighbor ball crossing at launch, near-horizontal pans.
4. Holdout access is rationed and logged with code hashes; tune and holdout are never pooled.
5. Thresholds are expressed physically (d0, frames, σ) so few free parameters exist. The tuned parameter count is logged.
6. As soon as real clips exist, label 30-50 with the existing contact-sheet tooling (both angles, 30/60 fps and slow motion, including BALL-01/02-style cases). They become the only acceptance gate for claims about real footage. Until then, the docs state "validated on synthetic data only".
