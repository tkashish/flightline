# D1: High-fidelity traces by evolving Tracking V2 (incremental)

Branch `high-fidelity-traces` (from origin/feat/automatic-tracking-v2 @ fc84dad, `npm test` 60 pass). Design philosophy: **incremental**. Keep v2's shape (MotionTracker front end → global beam association → track-status), fix each verified root cause where it lives, add as little new architecture as possible, and keep `?engine=legacy` and `?engine=v2` available. Validation is synthetic only. All numbers below come from scratch experiments in `/tmp/flightline-understand/design/D1-scratch/` (Appendix A). The repo was not modified.

---

## 1. Summary

- Evolve v2 in place: keep MotionTracker, `global-associate.js` and `track-status.js`, and add four small modules (`capture.js`, `launch-gate.js`, a rewritten `roi-evidence.js` + `roi-track.js`, `flight-model.js`) behind a new engine id `v2hf`. `legacy` and `v2` stay selectable.
- **Key idea A: impact-anchored launch identity.** The ball is the *last* stationary object to leave the address, and it leaves on the club's *final* fast pass. For its first frames it obeys a projective-line law: position `s(τ)=vτ/(1+cτ)` along a straight line from the address, apparent width `D0/(1+cτ)` with the *same* `c`, and a start time `t0` inside the impact interval. Club glints, the club head, hands and glove, takeaways and practice swings all violate it. Only this identity may seed flight beams.
- **Key idea B: every frame, native pixels, physical completion.** Capture uses playback + `requestVideoFrameCallback` + `VideoFrame`, which gives exact presentation timestamps, no duplicate frames, and analyzes a 10 s 4K60 clip in 1.0-2.0x its duration. Native-resolution ROI crops are taken along a physically predicted path and scored with multi-frame matched filtering. A camera-frame ballistic fit (depth from the address-ball size) smooths the track *without dropping observations* and completes the arc to landing or frame exit, with per-point status `observed | interpolated | predicted`.
- The legacy-link fallback is deleted. A single gate decides abstention: confirmed launch + association evidence + fit quality.
- Synthetic evidence. Down-the-line (DTL) launch identity is 97-98% correct on held-out seeds and a held-out render style (shipped v2 produces a correct full-ball track on only 16-20% and tracks the club on 5-9%). Practice-swing false launches: 0/192. Impact timing: median ≤ 0.15 frame. Native crops raise detection of 1.2-1.8 px balls in 4K from 8-29% to 87-97%. Physics completion error is 1-9% of frame height on DTL arcs once 25-50% of the visible flight is observed; drivers need ≥ 50%, and at 25% their error is 17-21%. All three association-steal reproductions are fixed (12/12, 34/34, 10/11).
- Main open risk: face-on clips with long exposure. On the held-out style, correct launches fall to 33% and abstentions rise to 41%. This needs the streak mode in §3.6 and §4.2 before the face-on targets can be met.

## 2. End-to-end architecture and data flow

### 2.1 Passes

```
MAIN THREAD                                         WORKER (module, one per clip)
P0 probe (≈0.4 s): metadata, rVFC fps/Δ,
   capability flags, deliveredHz; optional
   async audio decode → impact onsets          ──► init {analysisW,H, sourceW,H, fps, Δ, caps, focalPrior, onsets?}
P1 capture @ rate r (muted playback)
   rVFC(mediaTime) → new VideoFrame(video)     ──► vframe {frame, mediaTime, index}
   back-pressure: pause when >3 in flight      ◄── released (sent right after readback)
                                                    1 readback: createImageBitmap(frame,{resize}) → RGBA 720×1280
                                                    2 native ROI crops: frame.copyTo(rect) → luma (+UV)
                                                    3 frame.close()
                                                    4 MotionTracker.detect (v2hf front end, no sky coaster)
                                                    5 LaunchGate.observeFrame (spots, pass energy, address ring)
                                                    6 RoiEvidence on crops → candidates with z / LLR → frameLog
P2 decide                                       ──► finish
                                                    LaunchGate.finalize → associateFlight (v2.1)
                                                    → fitFlight → completion plan → recovery windows?
P3 recovery (bounded, optional)                 ◄── needRecovery {windows:[{t0,t1}], frames:[mediaTime…]}
   replay windows @ r2, or seek-fill frame centres ─► vframe {…, recover:true} → model-guided ROIs + stacks
P4 finalize                                     ──► finalize → re-associate, refit, completeArc → result
Render/export: buildTraceCurve(result.points) → existing drawTrace / MediaRecorder export (unchanged contract)
```

- **P1 is the only full pass.** Every presented frame is decoded once at native resolution by the media pipeline. We read back a downscaled analysis frame plus a few small native crops, and the `VideoFrame` is closed within about 6-8 ms.
- **The online launch gate is provisional.** As soon as an address spot vanishes (confirmed `N_gone` frames later), it starts "launch watch" crops. A ring of native address crops guarantees that the frames *before* confirmation are still available. When a provisional launch passes, it drives online follow ROIs along the launch law. The final decision is made at `finish`.
- **P3 recovery.** It re-reads only (a) frames missing from P1 inside the flight window (mediaTime gaps), (b) frames inside the observed span where the fitted model predicts an in-frame ball with no observation, and (c) frames after the last observation while predicted visibility is ≥ 0.1. It uses prediction ± 3σ ROIs, is capped by the time budget, and runs at most twice.

### 2.2 Worker protocol (backward compatible)

| message | payload | reply |
|---|---|---|
| `init` | `{width,height}` (analysis), `sourceWidth, sourceHeight, fps, frameDuration, engine:'legacy'\|'v2'\|'v2hf', caps, focalPrior, audioOnsets?, diagnostics?` | `{ok}` |
| `vframe` (new) | `{frame: VideoFrame (transferred), mediaTime, presentedFrames?, index, recover?:bool}` | `{type:'released'}` immediately after readback; `{type:'progress', processed}` every 10 frames |
| `bitmap` (new fallback) | `{bitmap: ImageBitmap, mediaTime, index}` | same as `vframe` |
| `frame` (kept) | `{buffer: RGBA ArrayBuffer, time}`, used by the legacy path, Node harness and tests | `{ok}` |
| `finish` | `{}` | `{phase:'needRecovery', windows, frames, budgetSec}` **or** the final result |
| `finalize` (new) | `{}` | final result |

`roi-*` and `refine-*` handlers are removed (dead code, v2-frames-I6). Unknown types reply with `{error}` instead of timing out (v2-frames-I7).

### 2.3 Result contract (renderer/export compatible)

```js
result = {
  points: [{ t, x, y, status: 'launch'|'observed'|'interpolated'|'predicted'|'manual',
             sigma?, alpha?, obs?: {x, y} }],   // normalized, camera-stabilized; x,y = drawn (fitted) position
  camera: [{ t, x, y }],                       // unchanged
  engine: 'v2hf', status: 'tracked'|'partial'|'no_reliable_track', reasonCodes: [...],
  observedFrames, predictedFrom: t_lastObserved, arcEnd: 'landing'|'exit'|'fade'|'clip-end',

```

Old consumers that read `{t,x,y}` still work. `observedFrames` counts only `observed` points.

**Policy (10 s 4K60 target: ≤ 2x).** P1 runs at 1x for 60 fps sources and 2x for ≤ 30 fps sources when deliveredHz ≥ 58. P3 replays the flight window at `r2 = deliveredHz / fps`. The scheduler caps P1+P3 at 1.9x clip duration and drops the lowest-value recovery frames first (late, sub-visible ones). Expected totals:

- 1.05-1.4x on an idle laptop;
- 1.6-1.9x with a 30 Hz compositor;
- 2.5-4x on recent phones, where back-pressure slows the pass without dropping analysis frames.

The current app needs ≥ 3x for 4K60 and analyzes only half the frames (plus a third duplicates on 30 fps clips).

## 3. Launch, impact and club-head rejection

### 3.1 Units and notation

Thresholds are expressed only in these units:

- `D0`: apparent address-ball diameter, measured at **native** resolution as the minor-axis width `4√λ_min` of the half-contrast blob in an address-ring crop.
- `F`, `Δ = 1/F`: source frame rate and frame duration, from rVFC.
- `H`: frame height in px of the same space.
- frame counts `k`, video time `t`, and dimensionless ratios.

Real-world speeds enter only through a phone focal-length prior (`f ≈ 0.5·H / tan(35°)` for the 1x lens; σ_log 0.25) and the address depth `Z0 = f·0.0427 m / D0`. Slow motion is never assumed: every test is either frame-based or invariant to time scaling (ratios, straightness, `a/v²`).

### 3.2 Address spots

A spot is a coarse candidate that persists at the same camera-stabilized position. A spot is an **address candidate** when all of these hold:

| rule | threshold |
|---|---|
| match tolerance across frames | ≤ max(1.5 px, 0.3·D) center distance, size ratio ∈ [0.4, 2.5], gaps ≤ max(2, ⌈0.08 s·F⌉) frames |
| dwell | ≥ `N_addr` = max(6, ⌈0.25 s·F⌉) frames **and** ≥ 0.5 × the longest dwell among candidates (rule R0: mid-swing pauses are short at any slow-motion factor) |
| shape | roundness ∈ [0.6, 1.6], fill ≥ 0.5, low chroma; either polarity |
| placement | not in the top 25% of the frame; outside the predicted-sky band if a horizon was found |
| arrival veto | no moving candidate at distance (0.5, 4)·D within 3 frames *before* the spot first appears (a body part that arrived and paused, e.g. hands or glove at the top of the backswing) |

Implementation: `tracker.js updateSpots` keeps its history. The `v2hf` path also records per-spot `{first, last, n, D_native, shape stats}` and feeds them to `LaunchGate`.

### 3.3 Vanish events and the club's passes

1. **Vanish.** The spot is absent for ≥ `N_gone` = max(3, ⌈0.1 s·F⌉) frames. If a new spot later appears at the same position, the object returned (waggle or nudge) and this is not a vanish.
2. **Pass energy.** Online, for every address candidate, the worker computes camera-stabilized frame-difference energy `E(k) = Σ|g_k − g_{k−1}|` (pixels with |d| > 10) in two disks: small `r_s = max(3·D, 0.02·H)` and large `r_l = max(4·D, 0.06·H)`. "Runs" are contiguous segments with `E > max(25·median(E), 0.05·max E)`. "Strong" runs have a peak ≥ max(50·median, 0.1·max). This uses energy rather than coarse movers on purpose: the held-out style showed that long faint blur streaks never become coarse candidates.
3. **R5, final fast pass.** A vanish is an impact candidate only if (a) it lies within ±1 frame of a strong small-disk run, and (b) no strong run in either disk starts more than max(3, ⌈0.3 s·F⌉) frames after the end of that run. Reason: a club head, ferrule or glint that vanishes at takeaway is always followed by the club's return to the same place about 1 s (real time) later. The ball leaves on the last pass.
4. **R1, ordering.** If two vanishes fall in one address cluster (≤ 4·D apart) and the later one comes ≥ ⌈0.3 s·F⌉ frames after the earlier, only the later can be the ball. The club leaves first.
5. **Practice swings** (no ball, or the ball left in place) yield no impact candidate, so the status is `no_launch`.

### 3.4 Impact interval and the optional audio prior

The impact interval is `[t_lastSeen − Δ/4, t_firstAbsent + Δ/4]`. The departure fit (§3.5) refines `t0` inside it.

Audio is optional. The main thread `fetch`es the object URL, calls `decodeAudioData`, and the `audio-onset.js` detector runs on it. The detector computes high-passed (>2 kHz) energy with a 1 ms hop and spectral flux, and picks transients with rise time < 3 ms. An onset adds a Gaussian prior on `t0` (σ = Δ/2) only when it falls within 1.5·Δ of the visual interval. It never creates an impact by itself and never vetoes one. If decoding fails or the audio is silent or stretched (slow motion), it is ignored.

### 3.5 Departure law and acceptance tests

Inputs come from frames `k_last+1 … k_last+L` with L = max(4, ⌈0.03 s·F⌉):

- coarse candidates that are not persistent spots;
- native candidates from the launch-watch crops (address ring plus crops around coarse movers within 0.5·H of A);
- for each candidate: centroid, minor and major width, orientation and polarity measured at native resolution.

**Chain building.** For each first point `p1` in frames `k_last+1…k_last+2`, define a departure band: perpendicular distance ≤ max(2.5 px, 0.35·D0) + 0.04·(distance along the band). Enumerate chains of up to 4 points (one per frame, frames may be skipped) and fit:

```
p(τ) = A + d·v·τ/(1+cτ),   w(τ) = D0/(1+cτ),   τ = t − t0,
t0 ∈ impact interval, v > 0, 0 ≤ c ≤ c_max = (80 m/s / Z0) (real time; slow motion only lowers it)
cost = RMS(along-line residual)/max(2, 0.05 L) + 1.5·mean|ln(w_i(1+cτ_i)/D0)| − 0.35·n
```

`c ≈ 0` is a lateral (face-on) launch and `c > 0` a receding (DTL) launch. One law covers both views; the view is an output, not an input.

**Acceptance.** All of these must hold:

| test | rule | what it rejects |
|---|---|---|
| straight | max perpendicular residual ≤ max(2.5 px, 0.35·D0) + 0.04·L | club-head arcs (face-on), glove arcs |
| size law | mean \|ln(w_i(1+cτ_i)/D0)\| ≤ ln 1.6 | glints and ferrule (≪ D0), club head (≫ D0, elongated) |
| start | w1 ∈ [0.6, 2.2]·D0 and 1 + c·τ1 ≤ 1.6 | small shrinking specks fitted with a large `c` |
| n ≥ 3 | RMS ≤ max(1.5 px, 0.05·L); v ≥ 15·D0 per video-second; consecutive speed ratio ≤ 1.25 | takeaways and downswing starts. They accelerate from rest: a real-time takeaway averages ≈ 3-7 D0/s over its first 0.1 s, while 8x slow-motion DTL chips measured 37-40 D0/s (E3) |
| n ≤ 2 (fast face-on exits) | first jump ≥ 2·D0; **leader**: no co-moving object within 45° of the direction has a distance ≥ 1/1.3 of the ball's unless it is within 1.5·D0 of the ball; \|ln(w1/D0)\| ≤ ln 1.5; if the blob is elongated (major/minor ≥ 1.6) its axis is within 20° of the A→p direction | club head right behind the ball. After impact the ball is 1.5-2.3× faster than the club head: smash 1.1-1.5 divided by the club's 0.65-0.75 post-impact speed retention |
| no arrival continuation | no pre-impact mover behind A on the same line whose departure/arrival speed ratio is in (0.45, 1.05) | club parts passing straight through the address (face-on) |

**Decision.**

- The lowest-cost passing chain wins → `launch`.
- If another passing chain from a different event, or with a direction differing by > 0.2 (unit-vector distance), is within 1 cost unit → `ambiguous_launch`.
- No passing chain → `no_ball_evidence`. No impact candidate → `no_launch`.

The result is a `LaunchDecision`: `{status, A, D0, t0, interval, dir, v, c, obs[], margin, features, swingMask}`.

- `swingMask` contains candidate ids from pre-impact mover tracks within 0.35·H of A, plus ids from chains rejected by the arrival or leader tests. These are inadmissible for flight beams in `[t0 − 0.15 s·κ̂, t0 + 0.3 s·κ̂]`.
- `κ̂` is the slow-motion factor estimated from the takeaway-run-to-impact interval (real backswing + downswing ≈ 1.0-1.3 s). It is used only as a prior.

### 3.6 Streak mode (face-on, long exposure; required by the held-out failure)

In the launch-watch crops, run an oriented line filter around every blob with major/minor ≥ 1.6. The kernel is a box of width D0 and length L along the blob's axis. Keep streaks whose extension passes within 0.5·D0 of A.

- The streak's near end and far end are the positions at exposure start and end. Their midpoint is the observation at the frame time.
- `w` = the streak width, which must still obey the size law.
- `exposure·F = streak length / frame displacement` must be constant across the clip's streaks. This is a consistency check, not a club discriminator.

### 3.7 Coverage of the hard cases

| case | behaviour |
|---|---|
| DTL | Ball and club directions differ by ~10-20°. The size law (`c > 0`, the ball shrinks) and the start test separate them. On synthetic data: 97-98% correct launches, 1.6-3.1% club. |
| face-on | The club head travels the ball's line at about half its speed. The leader rule, arrival continuation and size law separate them. 1-2 observations before the exit are allowed. |
| slow motion (4x / 8x) | Every rule is frame-count or ratio based. The dwell rule R0 removes top-of-backswing pauses, which last 15-24 frames at 4x. |
| practice swing | No vanish on the final pass → `no_launch`. 0/192 synthetic false launches (shipped v2 traced something on 6-17%). |
| multiple stationary balls, tees | They never vanish, or vanish without a final pass. A tee left behind sits ≈ 0.5-0.7·D0 below the ball center, so the ball spot does not re-match it. A flying tee fails the start-width test. |
| ball occluded by the club at impact | Chains may skip one frame. The interval widens by one frame. |
| ball not detectable at address | `no_launch`. Honest abstention; manual adjust remains available. |

## 4. Flight tracking of a faint, small ball

### 4.1 Where to look (ROI planner, `roi-track.js`)

Up to 4 ROIs per frame, in native pixels, with even-aligned rectangles (chroma), side = clamp(2·(3σ_pred + 2·D(t) + 8), 64, 320):

1. launch-watch and address-ring crops (§3.5);
2. the provisional launch's online follow: prediction from the launch law for the first 4 frames, then quadratic least squares over the last ≤ 8 observations;
3. at most one runner-up hypothesis;
4. in P3, the fitted flight model's prediction ± 3σ for each recovery frame.

### 4.2 Measuring at native resolution (`roi-evidence.js` v2)

- **Input.** The luma plane (Y of NV12/I420) from `VideoFrame.copyTo(rect)`, with no RGB conversion and no resampling. UV at half resolution provides a low-saturation check. 10-bit HDR planes are normalized by robust statistics, never by absolute thresholds.
- **Background.** Temporal median of the last 5 registered crops of the same hypothesis. Registration uses the global sub-pixel camera offset scaled to native px, plus optional local phase correlation when the crop has texture. Until 5 crops exist, a spatial 11×11 box minus the core is used. This removes static sky, clouds, trees and netting, and avoids the ghosting of v2-roi-I4.
- **Matched filter.** Gaussian/LoG on the residual at σ_mf = max(0.7, D(t)/2.5) px, for both polarities. The polarity prior comes from the last 3 observations; when polarity is unknown, both are tested and a Bonferroni penalty is applied.
- **CFAR.** `z = (resp − median)/(1.4826·MAD)` over the ROI with 3σ cores excluded. Single-frame threshold `z1 ≈ 4.5-5` (E2 calibration: 1% false alarms per gated search after x264). This replaces the absolute `minVisual` and fixes v2-roi-I6.
- **Track-before-detect.** For the predicted velocity ± 0.5 px/frame (grid 0.25, scaled by |v̂|·Δ), sum `z` along the path over a trailing K = 5 frames: `z_K = Σz/√K` (threshold ≈ 4.3-5.4 at 1% false alarms). Acceleration-aware paths come from the model prediction. K = 9 hurt bright balls in E2 because of constant-velocity mismatch, so the detector reports `max(single, K=5)`.
- **Sub-pixel position** by a parabolic fit on the matched-filter peak. **Streak mode** as in §3.6 whenever the predicted blur length (from the clip's measured `exposure·F`) exceeds 1.6·D(t).
- **Output.** `{x, y (native, stabilized), z, llr, width, major, angle, polarity, streak, origin:'roi'}`. Scores are **not** brightness-weighted: `llr ≈ z²/2 − ln N_hyp`, which keeps them on one scale with coarse candidates (fixes v2-roi-I2/I3/I5).
- **Evidence (E2/E2b).** With 4K sources, single-frame Pd for 1.2-1.8 px balls is 87-97% at native resolution versus 8-29% on the 3x-downscaled analysis frame. In the marginal regime (x264 at 0.15 bpp), the 5-frame stack lifts Pd by 25-40 points: d 0.8 px, C 45 from 5% to 38%; d 1.2 px, C 25 from 14% to 53%; dark d 1.8 px on overcast from 16% to 53%. It cannot rescue the faintest cases (d 0.8 px, C 25: 0% to 8%).

### 4.3 Global association (`global-associate.js` v2.1)

| change | verified defect it fixes |
|---|---|
| Launches come only from `LaunchDecision` (deduplicated: one physical launch, one id). Legacy and online tracks enter as *candidates* (`framesFromTracks`), never as launches. | duplicate launch ids → false `ambiguous_launch` (v2-tracker-I4); cold start without hints (v2-global-I6) |
| Seeds only at `t ≥ t0`, within 3 frames, with `v`, `dir`, `c` from the launch law | 0.2 s of pre-impact address points (v2-frames-I8) |

| Score = Σ(llr_i − ½·residual²/σ²) + Σ_misses ln(1 − Pd_pred). No `log1p(n)` length reward. Null = 0; confirmation needs ≥ τ_conf (calibrated). | length rewards coasting onto clutter (v2-global-I2, I5) |
| **Reserved quota**: the best 4 beams of every live launch survive pruning; the rest are filled by score | later real launch evicted at birth (v2-global-I3). E5: 12/12 correct vs 8/12, including at beam width 400 |

| Stump rule (climb < 25 px or span < 40 px) removed | rejecting complete arcs that land low (v2-global-I5) |
| `swingMask` candidates inadmissible near impact | club re-capture after a face-on exit |
| Coasting is dynamic: continue while σ_pred ≤ max(6·D(t), 0.04·H) and the prediction is in frame; stop at frame exit (`exited`) | fixed maxMiss = 8 frames is not time-scale invariant |

### 4.4 Camera motion (`tracker.js cameraShift` → `cameraShiftRobust`)

- **Estimator.** Coarse-to-fine block matching on the analysis gray: quarter scale ±6 px, then half scale ±2, then full scale ±1. A parabolic sub-pixel fit follows. The 6×10 grid of 48 px blocks uses sparse sampling.
- **Robust fusion.** Blocks with low texture or high temporal residual (golfer, club, ball ROIs) are discarded. The shift is the median over the remaining blocks, with confidence = fraction of agreeing blocks. Cost ≈ 2-4 ms.
- **Low confidence (< 0.3)** holds the previous offset and flags the frame `camera_uncertain`. Spot histories are never wiped. This fixes odd-pixel drift and the low-texture wipe-out (tracker-detection-I4).
- **Native ROIs** use the same offset scaled by `1/scale`, optionally refined locally.
- **Contract.** It stays translation-only. Rotation from handheld shake (≤ 0.3° on phones) is absorbed by the ROI-local refinement.

### 4.5 Sky vs ground, occlusion, frame exit, slow motion

- **Background-dependent visibility.** Per-ROI MAD and the polarity history feed `Pd_pred(t)`, which sets miss costs and recovery priority. Over trees, a white ball becomes high-contrast bright. Against bright overcast sky it may be darker: polarity flips are allowed, with a penalty.
- **Horizon.** A per-column sky/ground boundary from frame 0 (vertical gradient plus color) gives the face-on ground row and the DTL horizon for the landing sanity check.
- **Occlusion.** Misses are allowed while σ stays bounded. Golfer occlusion in the first DTL frames is handled by chain skips.
- **Frame exit.** When the prediction leaves the frame by more than D(t), the track ends with `exit` and completion runs to the edge (§5).
- **Slow motion.** All gates scale with predicted displacement per frame, so slow-motion clips simply get more frames per metre of flight and therefore more integration.

### 4.6 Identity steals and their guards

| intruder | guard |
|---|---|
| club head, shaft, glints, glove | launch law, swingMask, leader rule (§3) |
| static speck, sign, flag, lens dust | static-persistence label |
| airplane | pre-impact mover label; change point; physics fit residual |
| bird | change point; polarity and size consistency (a DTL ball shrinks monotonically; flapping birds change size); physics fit residual |
| another player's ball | no launch from our address; pre-impact mover if earlier; change point if it crosses later |
| sky noise (former coaster fabrication) | coaster removed from `v2hf`; CFAR plus multi-frame evidence; miss costs |

### 4.7 Removed from `v2hf` (kept for `legacy` / `v2` A/B)

Sky coaster (`localFlightCandidates*`). Steepness gates (seed rise/dist ≥ .55, early link |vx| ≤ 1.15|vy|, riseRate ≥ .8). The motion-blur-hostile seed quality gate (`p.quality ≥ max(.75, spot.quality−.12)`; blurred balls measured 0.65-0.73 in E3). Size-dominated `identityScore` / `launchPrior` ranking. The `result()` glare and hook trims. The v2 stump rule. The legacy-link fallback, which is removed from **all** engines.

## 5. Trajectory model, smoothing and full-arc completion (`flight-model.js`)

### 5.1 Models

- **M_img**: a per-axis quadratic in video time. Used for face-on and other short-horizon segments to the frame exit.
- **M_phys**: a camera-frame ballistic model.
  - Launch point `P0` on A's ray at depth `Z0 = f·0.0427/D0`.
  - Dynamics `a = g_cam − k_d|v|v + k_l|v|²·L̂(side)`: gravity direction from the fitted camera pitch (roll prior 0 ± 3°), quadratic drag, Magnus lift perpendicular to v and rotated by a side angle (fade, draw, slice, hook).
  - Unknowns: V0 (3), k_d, k_l, side, pitch, log κ (time scale), and optionally a log focal scale.
  - Priors: k_d 0.0055 ± 0.0035, k_l 0.0045 ± 0.0035 (1/m), side 0 ± 0.35 rad, pitch 0.08 ± 0.12 rad, log κ from κ̂ (σ 0.5; 0.05 when real time is certain), log f-scale 0 ± 0.25.
  - Integration: semi-implicit Euler at 1/480 s (real time).

### 5.2 Fitting (robust)

- **M_phys initialization**: a linear least-squares solve for V0 from ray constraints, `(P0 + V0·t + ½g t²) × r_i = 0`, on the first ≤ 10 observations.
- **Optimization**: multi-start on κ ∈ {0.7, 1, 1.4}·κ̂, then Levenberg-Marquardt with central differences, Huber weights (k = 2σ_meas) and the priors. Cost ≈ 30 ms (measured).
- **Model selection** is by forward validation: fit on the first 75% of observations and score on the last 25%. Use M_img when n < 8. In E4 this picked the better of M_img and M_phys in every configuration.
- **Outliers**: residual > 3σ after the robust fit is flagged `rejected`. Rejected points stay in diagnostics and are not drawn. **No interior observation is dropped otherwise**.
- **Drawn curve**: the model plus a penalized smoothing spline of the residuals, with λ chosen so the correction's RMS ≤ σ_meas. The curve therefore passes within ~σ of every inlier. This replaces `polishFlight` / `denoiseFlight` for `v2hf`. `smoothFlight` is kept for legacy.
- **Manual points** (`manual`) are hard constraints: weight ×100 and never rejected. Manual adjust triggers a refit and re-completion. This fixes ui-render-export-I1, where smoothing could discard corrections.

**E4 accuracy.** Mean error over predicted in-frame points, % of frame height, median over trials:

| view / source | fraction of visible flight observed | M_img | projective quad | **M_phys** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DTL 1080p60, iron / wedge / punch | 25% | 92-96 | unstable | 2.8-9.5 |
| DTL 1080p60, iron / wedge / punch | 50% | 85-93 | 5-80 | 4.1-6.4 |
| DTL 1080p60, driver | 25% / 50% / 100% | 89-97 | 7-100 | 16.9 / 4.7 / 4.5 |
| DTL 4K60, all shots | 25% / 50% | 79-94 / 36-84 | 7-100 | 4.4-5.5 / 0.8-3.5 |
| face-on (landscape 6 m, or 8x slow motion portrait), to the exit | 25-50% | **0.2-1.8** | unstable | 1.6-13, with divergent fits in some 8x cases |

Error at the landing point is larger: median 3-36% of H in DTL 1080p and 0.2-15% in 4K. That is why the end of the arc fades with uncertainty (§5.3) instead of always committing to a landing.

### 5.3 Landing and fade, per view

- **View** comes from the launch law plus the fit: `c·Δ` large and receding → DTL; `c ≈ 0`, lateral → face-on; otherwise oblique (treated like DTL).
- **DTL / oblique (M_phys).** Integrate until the predicted ball returns to the ground plane through the address (normal from the fitted pitch and roll) → `landing` at t_L. Propagate the parameter covariance to the image: σ(t).
  - The arc fades (alpha 1 → 0 over 0.35 s of video) starting at the earlier of t_L − 0.35 s, or the time σ(t) exceeds 4% of H → `fade`.
  - The horizon row is a sanity bound: a predicted landing more than 3% of H above the horizon is clipped to a fade.
- **Face-on (M_img).** Extend to the frame edge plus D(t) → `exit`. Short chips that land in frame land at the ground row through A (corrected for roll).
- **Clip ends before t_L.** Default `complete-at-end`: compress the remaining predicted time into the last 0.6 s of the clip, so exports show the full TV-style arc. `clip-end` is recorded and diagnostics hold the true model times. The setting `hold` instead draws the remainder instantly at the last frame.

### 5.4 Predicted points and visual treatment

- **Statuses**: `launch` is A at t0. `interpolated` covers launch → first observation and internal gaps. `observed` points sit at fitted positions, with the raw measurement in `obs`. `predicted` points come after the last observation, at source frame times, with `sigma` and `alpha`. `manual` marks user points.
- **Stroke**: one continuous stroke. The renderer (`trace-curve.js`) draws a Catmull-Rom curve through the given points at 120 Hz with no further polishing, applies `alpha` at the tail, and keeps the head dot at the current time, observed or predicted (TV look).
- **Debug**: a `?debug=arc` toggle dashes predicted segments.
- **Export**: unchanged, apart from using the new curve.
- **README wording** replaces "nothing fabricated": "Observed points are measured. The rest of the arc is predicted from a physical fit and stored with `status: 'predicted'`; the tracer draws both as one arc and the app reports how much was seen."

## 6. Abstention and confidence

### 6.1 Gate (one place: `resultHF` → `track-status.js`)

| condition | status | reason codes |
|---|---|---|
| no address object left on the club's final fast pass | `no_reliable_track` | `no_launch` |
| impact found, no departure passes §3.5 | `no_reliable_track` | `no_ball_evidence` |
| two physically distinct passing departures within margin | `no_reliable_track` | `ambiguous_launch` |
| launch passes, but association score < τ_conf and < 3 observations (1-2 allowed only for leader-confirmed face-on exits) | `no_reliable_track` | `insufficient_visible_frames` |
| ≥ 3 observations, fit RMS ≤ max(1 px, 0.25·D) native, no unresolved change point, σ at arc end ≤ 8% H | `tracked` | `arc_completed` (info) |
| as above but σ grows fast: the arc fades early | `tracked` | `arc_faded` (info) |
| identity confirmed but fit ill-conditioned (fewer than 3 observations not exiting, or RMS too high) | `partial` (observed points + ≤ 0.25 s prediction or until σ > 2% H) | `tracking_ended_early` |
| camera confidence < 0.3 on > 30% of flight frames | `partial` | `camera_motion_uncompensated` |
| > 20% of flight-window frames still missing after recovery | `partial` | `decoder_limited` |

**Arc completion requires a confirmed identity.** No `predicted` point is emitted unless status is `tracked` with launch status `launch`.

### 6.2 What replaces the broken fallback

Nothing bypasses the gate. The legacy linker's tracks become candidates; its launches become nothing. The reasons the fallback existed are fixed at their source:

- cold start → launch-law velocity;
- launch stumps → stump rule removed and launch confirmed;
- pre-impact seeds → removed;
- evictions → quotas.

`?engine=legacy` still returns the old linker for A/B comparisons. It is never mixed into `v2hf` output.

### 6.3 Confidence

- **Model**: a logistic model fitted on the synthetic tune split, evaluated on the hold-out (reliability diagram, ECE ≤ 0.05).
- **Features**: launch-cost margin, n observed, association score, fit RMS/σ, σ at arc end, ambiguity gap, camera confidence, fraction of missing frames.
- **Reported**: `displayScore` with `calibrated: 'synthetic-v1'` (not real-world calibrated), plus a separate `arcCertainty` from σ at the arc end.
- **UI copy**: "Flight traced · 41 frames seen, rest predicted" and the confidence bar.
- **Bulk mode** auto-exports only `tracked` with displayScore ≥ 0.7. Everything else is listed for review.

## 7. Frame timing and scale invariance

### 7.1 Timing

- **fps.** P0 plays muted for about 0.4 s and collects rVFC `mediaTime`. Δ is the minimum positive delta, confirmed by the mode. VFR is flagged when deltas vary by > 10%. E1 recovered 30.00 and 60.00 exactly on all clips.
- **Timestamps** are `metadata.mediaTime` (equal to `VideoFrame.timestamp`), never `currentTime`.
  - A repeated mediaTime is a duplicate and is dropped.
  - A gap > 1.5·Δ means missing frames, which P3 re-fetches when they fall inside the flight window.
  - VFR clips use actual dt everywhere. Gates are displacement-based, so they need no fixed Δ.
- **Capture modes**, chosen by capability:
  1. `VideoFrame` playback (Chrome/Edge, Safari 16.4+, Firefox 130+);
  2. `createImageBitmap(video)` playback, with the bitmap transferred;
  3. seek fallback: targets at frame centres `t_i = t_first + (i + ½)·Δ`, each verified by rVFC metadata, with one retry at +Δ/2. In E1, center seeks had 0 repeats where `i/30` seeks had 100 of 300 on 30 fps clips. HEVC 4K60 still showed 7 of 120 repeats with center seeks, all detectable by mediaTime, hence the verification.
  
  Non-seekable media no longer needs the whole-clip cache, because playback needs no seeking (fixes v2-frames-I4).
- **Rate control.** r = clamp(deliveredHz/F, 0.25, 2), measured in P0 and adapted per second: halve r when > 5% of frames are missed. Back-pressure (> 3 frames in flight → `pause()`, resume at ≤ 1) protects slow devices without losing frames.
- **Slow motion.** κ̂ is estimated from the swing timeline (§3.5). It is used only as a prior for M_phys and for the completion duration. No gate depends on it.

### 7.2 Old gates → fps- and scale-invariant forms (`v2hf`)

| old (v2 / legacy) | new |
|---|---|
| fixed 30 Hz `i/30` sampling | every presented frame; mediaTime timestamps |
| `t − 1/30` seed restamp | `t0` from the launch law inside the impact interval |
| spot seen ≥ 8 samples, 2.5 px match | ≥ max(6, ⌈0.25 s·F⌉) frames + R0 dwell rule; match ≤ max(1.5 px, 0.3·D) |
| seed rise/dist ≥ .55; \|vx\| ≤ 1.15\|vy\|; riseRate ≥ .8 px/sample | none: direction-free projective-line law |
| seed quality ≥ max(.75, q − .12) | width ≈ D0 (size law); polarity consistency |
| link gap ≤ 5 / 8 / 12 samples; coast ≤ 12 samples | coast while σ_pred ≤ max(6·D(t), 0.04·H) |
| gatePx 55, posSigma 18 px, velSigma 90 px/s | 4σ, σ = 0.5 + 0.15·step + 0.25·misses (native px) |
| maxMiss 8 frames; seed window ±0.2 s | dynamic coasting; seeds only at `t ≥ t0` within 3 frames |
| default seed velocity (0, −120) px/s | `v, dir, c` from the launch fit |
| minObs 5; tracked ≥ 9 points; stump 25/40 px | launch confirmation + score τ_conf + fit quality |
| frameLog top 28 by quality | all candidates in launch-watch/ROI crops plus coarse top 48 |
| polishFlight drops "teleports" and kinks | robust model fit; only > 3σ outliers flagged |
| launchPrior / identityScore size terms | removed for `v2hf` (size enters only through the D0 law) |
| absolute ROI thresholds (minVisual 5) | CFAR z-scores and LLR |

  - libx265 `hvc1`;
  - MP4 or MOV with portrait rotation tags;
  - audio: ambience plus an impact click at t_imp + offset (0-80 ms), stretched or absent for slow motion.
- **Distractors**: birds (flapping, dark or light), airplanes (before or after impact), another bay's golfer and ball, people walking, lens dust (static specks), sun glare.
- **Truth** (`truth.json`, per presented frame by PTS):
  - ball: sub-pixel native position, apparent diameter, visibility (in frame, occluded, peak SNR after codec), phase (address, flight, landed, exited);
  - club parts, hands and glove; distractors with ids; camera transform;
  - impact time; landing time and image point; the full arc at 240 Hz, even past the clip end;
  - generator version and seed.
- **Rendering cost**: static layers are rendered once per clip, dynamic layers in dirty rectangles, and noise from precomputed tiles. Frames are piped to ffmpeg and never all held. Estimated 0.05-0.25 s per 1080p frame per core. A 1400-clip suite takes about 2-4 h on 12 cores; the CI smoke subset (40 clips) takes ≈ 5 min.

### 9.2 Scenario matrix

| factor | levels |
|---|---|
| view | DTL, face-on; oblique in the hold-out only |
| capture | 30 RT, 60 RT, 120→30 (4x), 240→30 (8x); 24 RT in the hold-out only |
| resolution | 1080×1920, 2160×3840; landscape 1920×1080 at 20% |
| ball faintness | from camera distance, lens, resolution and sky contrast; stratified by truth peak SNR: < 2.5, 2.5-5, ≥ 5 |
| sky / background | clear blue, hazy bright, overcast bright (dark-on-bright), broken clouds, trees high; sunset in the hold-out only |
| shot | driver, wood, 7-iron, wedge, chip, punch, topped/flat; fade, draw, slice, hook |
| confounders | glint 0-3; white glove or clothing; range balls 0-30; tee; birds 0-3; airplane (pre/post); other bay's shot; static specks 0-5; shake none/mild/strong; slow pan; exposure 1/120-1/4000; mishits |
| non-shots (10% of clips) | practice swing with no ball; practice swing beside a ball; waggle-only; two balls at address |

Continuous factors use Latin-hypercube sampling and categorical factors are stratified.

**Splits.**

- **tune**: 600 clips (seeds 10000-10599).
- **hold-out**: 600 clips (seeds 90000-90599), run only at milestones.
- **style hold-out**: 200 clips from generator style families never used in tuning: new textures and palettes, golfer proportions, the 0.5x lens, exposure ranges outside tune, 24 fps, oblique view, sunset.

### 9.3 Metrics (per clip, aggregated with Wilson 95% intervals, per stratum)

- **Club-head false-track rate**: share of shot clips where any of the first 4 observed points, or ≥ 10% of observed points, lie within 1.5·D0 of a club, hand or glove part and farther than D(t) from the ball.
- **Launch error**: |t0 − t_imp| in frames; |launch point − address| in D0. **Launch correct** = ≤ 1 frame, ≤ 0.5·D0, and the first 3 observations are the ball.
- **Identity correctness**: share of observed points within max(D(t), 2 px native) of truth and closer to the ball than to any distractor.
- **Observed recall**: share of truth-visible frames (in frame, unoccluded, D ≥ 0.5 px) with a correct observed point, reported per SNR bin.
- **Pixel error**: median and p95 native-px error of correct observed points, per SNR bin.
- **Arc-completion error**: mean distance of drawn `predicted` points to truth at equal t, as % of H, from the last observation to the truth landing/exit or clip end. Also the end-point error (% H), and *overshoot*: the drawn arc continuing more than 0.5 s past the truth landing or exit.
- **Abstention correctness**: non-shot → `no_reliable_track`; easy shot (≥ 6 truth frames at SNR ≥ 5) → not abstained.
- **Runtime and memory** (browser): wall time / clip duration; peak RSS increase.
- **Parity**: Node vs browser decision agreement and metric deltas.

## 10. Risks, what synthetic tests cannot prove, and limiting overfitting

**Risks**

1. **Face-on with long exposure or low contrast.** On the held-out style, the prototype fell to 33% correct launches (41% abstained, 4.7% club). Mitigation: §3.6 streak mode and energy-based activity. Face-on targets are gated separately.
2. **Lens prior.** The 0.5x ultra-wide halves f and therefore halves Z0, which skews M_phys completion. Mitigation: a log f-scale parameter in the fit, a forward-validation fallback to M_img, and fading on σ. The style hold-out includes 0.5x.
3. **Browser variance.** rVFC rate (throttled to 30 Hz under load in E1), `VideoFrame` and `createImageBitmap` resize support (Safari, Firefox), HDR/HLG frames (10-bit planes, tone mapping in canvas). Mitigation: capability probe, three capture modes, robust statistics, and a browser benchmark run per release.
4. **Phone EIS, digital zoom, auto-exposure jumps.** These cause non-rigid background motion and break the translation-only camera model. Mitigation: confidence flags, ROI-local registration, temporal-median backgrounds, and an abstention reason.
5. **Physics model limits.** Wind, mishits, roll, and κ errors in slow motion. Mitigation: priors, multi-start, forward validation, σ-based fades, and no completion without a confirmed identity.
6. **Performance on phones.** Estimated at 2.5-4x clip duration. Back-pressure keeps every frame but slows the pass. The ROI budget adapts (2 instead of 4 crops) when the per-frame time exceeds 25 ms.
7. **Hidden address ball** (deep grass, framed out, tee in shadow). The result is `no_launch`, which is honest but reduces recall. Manual adjust works again after the CSS fix.

**What synthetic tests cannot prove.** Real-world accuracy and confidence calibration. Real distractor statistics (club glint shapes, clothing, birds). Phone hardware encoder artifacts and HDR pipelines. Real AV offsets. Golfer variability. Whether users accept predicted arcs. They *can* prove logic correctness, invariance to fps, resolution and slow motion, the absence of known failure modes (club tracks, fallback leaks, steals, duplicates), and time and memory budgets in a real browser.

**Limiting overfitting to the generator**

- Every threshold is physically motivated, with its margin documented: smash and club-speed retention, takeaway speeds, the c bound from ball speed, CFAR false-alarm rates.
- Few free parameters, each with a sensitivity sweep in the tune report.
- Domain randomization of PSF, noise, codec, exposure and palette.
- Style families held out entirely; thresholds frozen before every hold-out run; hold-out run only at milestones.
- The tune → hold-out gap is tracked as an alarm (§9.4).
- An "adversarial" scenario set written by someone other than the tuner.
- The v2 annotation harness stays, extended with point statuses, so the first real clips can be labelled and scored as the next gate. The decision log's ML deferral (Phase 6) is unchanged.
