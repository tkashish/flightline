# D2: Flightline v3, an impact-anchored, two-pass, physics-constrained tracking engine

Design id: D2 (new engine). Branch: `high-fidelity-traces` (from `origin/feat/automatic-tracking-v2` at fc84dad).
Scratch evidence: `/tmp/flightline-understand/design/D2-scratch/` (all numbers below come from those scripts; no repo files were modified).

---

## 1. Summary

- v3 replaces the legacy front end (`tracker.js` link/seed/coaster) and the v2 beam association for launch and flight. It keeps the worker plumbing, `track-status.js`, render/export and the eval harness.
- **Decode:** a dependency-free MP4/MOV demuxer feeds WebCodecs in the worker, giving frame-exact PTS and one `copyTo` per frame (measured 9.6 ms per 4K frame). The fallback seeks the `<video>` to frame centres with rVFC (0 duplicates versus 1/3 for v2's `i/30` seeks).
- **Pass 1 (coarse):** finds the impact as a stationary address ball that departs while the swing passes over it; audio is an optional prior.
- **Launch:** an impact-anchored search tracks the club head through impact. It accepts only a small, new, perspective-consistent object that the club's arc does not explain and that beats self-calibrated null launches.
- **Pass 2 (native ROIs):** tracks the ball inside physics-predicted ROIs with track-before-detect, then runs a global Viterbi over cached evidence.
- **Fit and complete:** a projected 3-D golf-ball flight model (gravity, drag, Magnus lift, side spin, dimensionless priors) audits identity, smooths without dropping observations, and completes the arc to landing, frame exit or an uncertainty-limited fade.
- **Status:** every point is `anchor`, `observed`, `interpolated` or `predicted`. There is no legacy fallback: no confirmed ball identity means no trace.

**Key idea 1: the ball is what the club did not do.** The club head is tracked into impact and predicted out of it. Launch evidence is anchored to the address point and impact time and must agree with impact mechanics: ball ahead of the club (smash factor), inside a loft cone, shrinking consistently with perspective, and new in each frame. This targets "tracks the club head" at its root.

**Key idea 2: physics in the loop at native resolution.** One fitted flight model decides where to look (ROIs), what to accept (position, velocity, and size gates), how to integrate sub-pixel evidence (TBD along predicted paths), and how to finish the arc. This targets "does not track the ball completely": it extends observation further and completes the rest credibly.

## 2. End-to-end architecture and data flow

### 2.1 Passes

```
main thread (app.js)                         analysis worker (worker-v3.js -> dist/v3/engine.js)
--------------------                         ---------------------------------------------------
File --- {type:'v3-analyze', jobId, file} -> P0 PROBE   mp4-demux: moov via Blob.slice -> VideoInfo
                                                         (codec, coded WxH, rotation, PTS[], keyframes, fps, VFR, audio)
                                                         VideoDecoder.isConfigSupported?
                                                           yes -> Source A (WebCodecs, all work in worker)
<-- {type:'v3-need-frames'} ---------------    no  -> Source B (main thread seeks + rVFC, streams planes)
                                              P1 COARSE (every frame, luma at L1<=1280 long edge, chroma 1/2)
                                                 camera registration -> stabilised background -> stationary bank
                                                 -> motion blobs -> swing envelope -> sky/horizon -> FrameSummary (~2 KB)
                                              AUDIO (optional) AudioDecoder -> high-pass onset envelope -> clicks
                                              IMPACT  ImpactEvent[] (anchor, r_A, vanish frame, swing/audio support)
                                              P2 NATIVE (re-decode each event window from keyframe <= t_v - 0.25 s)
                                                 LaunchSearch (club chain, anchored peak-pair Hough, null launches)
                                                 -> FlightTracker (physics-predicted native ROIs, local TBD, evidence cache)
                                              GLOBAL (no decode) forward-backward Viterbi on cached evidence + robust refit
                                                 -> identity audit -> hypothesis selection vs null
                                              FIT + COMPLETE  projected-3D flight fit -> landing/exit/fade -> statuses
<-- {type:'v3-result', jobId, result} -----  RESULT dense 120 Hz curve + raw observations + camera + status/reasons/confidence
```

### 2.1a What is reused and what is replaced

| Reused | Replaced for v3 (kept only behind `?engine=v2\|legacy`) |
|---|---|
| Worker request/response pattern (`workerCall`, transfer lists); `track-status.js` (extended); `recordExport`/MediaRecorder/audio path; `cameraAt`; `tests/lib/metrics.mjs` for future real-clip annotations; contact sheets | Analysis sampling (`plannedSampleTimes` i/30 seeks); `MotionTracker` detection, seeding, link gates and coaster; `identityScore`; `associateFlight` beam search; `smoothFlight` polishing for automatic tracks; the `resultGlobal` legacy-link fallback |

### 2.2 Why two passes and not one

Impact is decided with global knowledge. That includes whether the ball ever comes back (occlusion versus departure), which of several stationary balls left, and whether a practice swing preceded the real one. Pass 2 then needs native-resolution pixels from slightly before impact onwards. Keeping 4K frames in a ring until impact is known would cost 12 MB of NV12 per frame and is not phone-safe. Re-decoding only the event windows costs less (section 2.6).

### 2.3 Frame sources

- **Source A (primary): WebCodecs in the worker.**
  - `dist/v3/mp4-demux.js` parses `moov`: `trak/mdia/mdhd` (timescale), `hdlr`, `tkhd` (matrix, which gives rotation), `stsd` (avcC/hvcC, giving the codec string and description), `stts/ctts/stss/stsc/stsz/stco/co64`, and `edts/elst`. It reads samples with `File.slice()` and never loads the whole file.
  - `VideoDecoder` runs with backpressure: `decodeQueueSize <= 8` and at most 3 live `VideoFrame`s.
  - Each frame is copied **once** with `VideoFrame.copyTo()` into a reused NV12/I420 buffer. Luma comes straight from the Y plane, the coarse image is a JS box-downscale, and ROIs are index arithmetic on the same buffer. Rotation is applied by index mapping.
  - `drawImage(VideoFrame)` is not used for analysis. In headless Chrome 155 it converts the full frame on every call: 30 ms for a 720×1280 draw and another 26 ms for a single 256×256 crop of a 4K frame (Appendix A).
- **Source B (fallback): `<video>` on the main thread.** Used when demux or decoder support fails, for example WebM in Safari, fragmented MP4, or unsupported HEVC.
  - Targets are frame centres, `t_i = PTS_i + Δ/2`, taken from demux PTS when available, otherwise from an rVFC fps probe.
  - Each sample's `t` is `requestVideoFrameCallback` metadata `mediaTime`. A repeated `mediaTime` triggers one retry nudged by `+Δ/4`.
  - The main thread draws to an L1 canvas and sends luma/chroma planes (pass 1). For pass 2 it answers ROI requests with `drawImage(video, sx, sy, sw, sh, …)` crops.
- **Source N (Node bench): ffmpeg.** `tests/bench/source-ffmpeg.mjs` decodes raw `gray` + `yuv420p` planes with `-fps_mode passthrough` and exact PTS from `ffprobe -show_frames`. The engine code is identical in Node and the browser, because the engine only sees `FrameRef` objects (section 8.1).

### 2.4 Worker protocol v3 (new `dist/worker-v3.js`; `worker.js` stays for `?engine=v2|legacy`)

| Direction | Message | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| main→worker | `{type:'v3-analyze', jobId, file:File, options:{diagnostics?, forceSource?:'video', audio?:true, longEdge1?:1280}}` | A File is structured-cloneable; the worker reads it by slices |
| worker→main | `{type:'v3-info', jobId, info:VideoInfo, source:'webcodecs'\|'needs-frames'}` | |
| worker→main | `{type:'v3-progress', jobId, phase:'probe'\|'pass1'\|'impact'\|'pass2'\|'global'\|'fit', done, total, note}` | Every 10 frames |
| worker→main (Source B) | `{type:'v3-need-frames', jobId, pass:1, plan:{indices[], targets[]}}`, later `{type:'v3-roi-request', jobId, index, t, rects:[{x,y,w,h,scale}]}` | ROI requests are lockstep |
| main→worker (Source B) | `{type:'v3-frame', jobId, pass:1, index, t, tSource:'rvfc'\|'target', y:ArrayBuffer, uv?:ArrayBuffer, w, h}` / `{type:'v3-roi', jobId, index, t, rois:[{rect, y:ArrayBuffer, uv?:ArrayBuffer}]}` | Transferred, no copy |

The main thread ignores any message whose `jobId` is not current. This fixes the cross-job clobbering races (v2-frames-I3, v2-ui-export-I5) without relying on abort ordering.

### 2.6 Time budget (measured where marked; M4 Pro, Chrome 155 headless)

| Stage | 10 s 1080p30 (300 frames) | 10 s 4K60 (600 frames) |
|---|---|---|
| Demux (moov parse) | 0.04 s (measured) | 0.07 s (measured) |
| Pass-1 decode + copyTo + downscale to 720×1280 | 300 × 4.0 ms = 1.2 s (measured) | 600 × 9.6 ms = 5.8 s (measured) |
| Pass-1 detection (registration 1.4, background 0.7, diff 0.8, DoG at half scale ~3, extrema 0.9 ms) | 300 × 7 ms = 2.1 s (estimated from a micro-benchmark; naive full-res DoG was 14.8 ms) | 600 × 7 ms = 4.2 s |
| Pass-2 re-decode of the event window (about 6.5 s incl. keyframe lead) | 200 × 3 ms = 0.6 s | 400 × 6 ms = 2.4 s |
| Launch search (native/¼-scale ROI, peak pairs) | ~0.2 s per event | ~0.3 s per event |
| Flight ROIs (evidence + TBD) | 200 × 2 ms = 0.4 s | 400 × 2.5 ms = 1.0 s |
| Global Viterbi + physics fit (scratch fit 0.2–0.5 s, unoptimised) + completion | ~0.5 s | ~0.5 s |
| **Total (laptop)** | **≈ 5 s (0.5× duration)** | **≈ 14 s (1.4× duration; target ≤ 2×)** |

Phones are estimated at 2–3× these numbers. An adaptive governor measures pass-1 throughput over the first 60 frames. If the projected total exceeds 2.5× clip duration, it drops L1 to 960 and processes pass 1 at stride 2 for 60 fps, while still decoding every frame. Pass 2 always runs at the full frame rate.

Source B is slower, because a seek costs 27.5 ms at 1080p and 87–99 ms at 4K (measured). That gives about 1.5× duration at 1080p30. At 4K60 it is about 5×, even with pass 1 at stride 2 and pass 2 at stride 2 beyond 1 s after impact. The UI shows a "slower decode path" notice.

## 3. Launch, impact and club-head rejection

**Units.**
- `Δ_k = t_k − t_{k−1}` is taken from PTS; `fps = 1/median Δ`.
- `W×H` is display size in native pixels and `D = hypot(W,H)`.
- `r_A` is the measured address-ball radius in native pixels.
- All evidence is in CFAR z units (noise- and clutter-normalised).
- Distances are in `r_A` or `D`; windows are in frames derived from `fps`. Nothing uses px/s at a fixed sample rate.

### 3.1 Address-ball candidates (pass 1, L1 resolution)

1. Camera registration (section 4.7) gives a stabilised frame. A scale-space DoG at `σ ∈ {1.0,1.4,2.0,2.8,4.0}` L1 px runs on the lower 80% of the frame. The small scales run at L1; the larger ones run on a 2× downsample, to stay inside the 7 ms budget. Detections are extrema with global-CFAR `|z| ≥ 4`; radius `r = √2·σ*`; position is sub-pixel by quadratic fit.
2. **Stationary bank** (≤64 entries). A detection matches an entry within `ε = 0.5 px@L1 + 0.25 r + 2σ_reg` in stabilised coordinates. Presence per frame means `z ≥ 0.5 × entry median z`. To survive odd-pixel drift and golfer-over-low-texture misregistration (tracker-detection-I4), each entry also keeps a 7×7 template that is matched locally (±3 px) every 4th frame. Stationarity is judged relative to the local static texture, not only the global camera.
3. **Address-ball candidate (ABC)** requirements:
   - present in ≥80% of frames over a run of ≥ `max(8 frames, 0.25 s)`;
   - `0.0015·H ≤ r_A ≤ 0.03·H` (native);
   - roundness (second-moment eigen-ratio) ≥ 0.6.

   Soft priors: bright polarity versus the `3r` annulus (0.9 prior; dark allowed), low chroma, `y ≥ 0.25·H`, and adjacency to the lower half of the swing envelope.
4. Native refinement: when pass 2 opens an event window, a native crop refines `p_A` and `r_A` (Gaussian fit). The U2 test target is ≤ 0.15 native px.

### 3.2 Impact events

For each ABC `a`, the presence signal `p_a(k)` is its normalised DoG response at its own position and scale.

- **Vanish:** `t_v` is the first frame after the stationary run where `p_a < 0.35`, provided it stays `< 0.5` for `N_gone = max(6 frames, 0.1 s)`.
- **Occlusion versus departure:** if `p_a` recovers to ≥0.7 at the same position within `max(18 frames, 0.6 s)`, this was an occlusion (waggle, practice swing, golfer's foot): **no event**.
- **Nudge:** a new ABC of similar size appearing within `12 r_A` within 1 s means the ball was moved, not launched: **no event**.
- **Swing support** `σ_s`: motion energy in a `3 r_A` disc around `p_A` during `[k_v−2, k_v+1]`, divided by its median over the preceding second. Support counts when this ratio is ≥4 (the club passes over the ball).
- **Audio support** `σ_a` (optional): the strongest onset within `±(1.5Δ + 80 ms)` of `t_v`. Onsets come from a 1.5 kHz high-pass, a 2.7 ms log-energy envelope, a positive-difference onset function, and peaks ≥ median + 6·MAD over 0.5 s. `σ_a` is 0 when audio is absent (typical in slow motion).
- **Score:** `S_e = 2·log(1 + 4·persistence_s) + 3·[σ_s ≥ 4] + min(2, log(σ_a/6)₊) + shape`.

Up to 3 events go to pass 2, in descending `S_e`.

- **Practice swings:** a swing over the ball without departure produces no event. A clip with only practice swings abstains with `no_impact`.
- **Multiple balls and tees:** every ABC is independent. Only the one that departs while the swing passes over it becomes an event.
- **Two real shots:** both are traced to confirmation; the higher-confidence one is shown and `multiple_shots` is reported.

### 3.3 Launch search (pass 2, first frames after impact)

**Processing window and scale.**
- Pass 2 starts decoding at the keyframe at or before `t_v − max(0.25 s, 12 frames)`.
- Launch window: `K_L = clamp(round(0.2 s × fps), 6, 16)` post-impact frames, plus 4 pre-impact frames.
- Processing scale: `s_L = clamp(3/r_A, 1/4, 1)`, so the address ball is about 3 px in radius. The launch ROI is the region reachable in `K_L` frames, clipped to the frame.

**Reference and evidence.**
- `B_ref` is the per-pixel median of 5 frames spread over `[t_v − 0.45 s, t_v − 0.15 s]`. The club and arms occupy any pixel in at most 1–2 of these frames, so the median removes them.
- Ball evidence for frame `j`:
  - residual `R_j = polarity·(Y_j − B_ref)`, rectified;
  - the **address hole** (disc of `3 r_A` around `p_A`) is zeroed for `j ≥ 1`. Without this, the DoG side-lobes of the hole win the search (seen in scratch);
  - DoG at `σ ∈ {0.8 r_A, 0.4 r_A}·s_L` (min 0.8 px);
  - **local CFAR** normalisation by clutter energy in the annulus `[2σ+1, 6σ+3]`. Without it, the golfer's residual edges saturate global z and win;
  - up to 14 peaks per frame with `z ≥ 3.5`, NMS radius `2 r_A`. Both per-scale maps are kept.

**Club-head chain.**
- Club evidence is local-CFAR DoG of `|Y_j − Y_{j−1}|` at `σ = 1.6 r_A`, top 8 peaks per frame.
- Choose `(c_{−1}, c_0)`, and optionally `c_{−2}`, so the head **arrives at `p_A` at impact with consistent speed**:
  - for impact phase `φ ∈ {0.15,0.4,0.65,0.9}` of the frame interval, `(|p_A−c_0|/φ) / |c_0−c_{−1}| ∈ [0.55, 1.8]`;
  - heading turn `cos ≥ 0.5`, step `≥ 2 r_A`;
  - optional `c_{−2}` step ratio in `[0.4, 1.6]` with `cos ≥ 0.4`.

  Requiring only two pre-impact frames matters: in portrait face-on the club head is often outside the frame three frames before impact.
- **Forward club tracker:** start with velocity `0.75×` the impact velocity (speed lost to the ball). Each frame, damp by 0.92, rotate by the measured turn rate, and snap to the nearest club peak within `max(5 r_A, 0.5|v|)`.
- Ball peaks inside the club-head extent `1.7 r_A + 1.5 px` (plus `0.06·d` if the club was not measured that frame) count as **club-explained**. Club glints and the club head are excluded this way. A ball separating from the club only 14–30 px in the first face-on frames is not excluded; the earlier `3 r_A + 0.12 d` radius removed the true ball.

**Hypotheses** (anchored at `p_A` and `t_i = t_{k0} + φΔ`):

```
d(τ) = v·τ / (1 + ρ·τ),   r(τ) = r_A / (1 + ρ·τ),   τ = (t − t_i)/Δ  (frames),  ρ ∈ [0, 2]
```

This is exact for constant 3-D velocity under perspective. Deceleration `ρ` and shrink are the **same** parameter, so a down-the-line ball must shrink as it slows. That one coupling rejects "converging" clutter paths that end on the golfer's body.

- Hypotheses come from **peak pairs** `(q1@j1, q2@j2>j1)` collinear with `p_A` within `max(2°, atan((1.5 r_A + 2)/d1))`, with `d2 > d1`. For each `φ`, solve the 2×2 system for `(v, ρ)`.
- Single-peak hypotheses come from frame 1 with `ρ = 0`.
- There is **no angle gate and no "must rise" gate**: flat face-on launches (5–15°) and descending-looking image paths are allowed. This fixes the ~34° launch-gate defect (`tracker.js:370,374–375,492`).

**Support** is evaluated for every in-frame `j ≤ K_L`. A peak supports the hypothesis if:
- it is within `1.5 px + 0.6 r_A + 0.03·d` of the prediction;
- it is **size-consistent**: if `r(τ) < 0.6 r_A`, then `z_big ≤ 1.25·z_small`; if `r(τ) > 0.85 r_A`, then `z_small ≤ 1.6·z_big`;
- it is **novel**: if the predicted step is `≥ 2.5 r(τ)`, `z_{j−1}` at that spot is ≤ `0.5·z_j`;
- it is **not club-explained**.

**Score:**

```
S = Σ_support w·(min(z,8) − 2) + Σ_unsupported in-frame (0.5·min(0, z_pred − 2) − 0.5)
w = 0.35 inside the pre-impact swing envelope, else 1
```

Missing evidence where the ball should be visible costs score.

**Reject a hypothesis if any of these hold:**
- `S < 3`;
- **precursor**: evidence along the constant-velocity backward extension over the 4 pre-impact frames is `> 0.5·max(1,S)`. Something already moving through the address is the club, a hand, or the body;
- one hit while ≥2 in-frame frames were predicted;
- **outrun the club**: the hypothesis travels within 30° of the club's measured post-impact direction, yet its first support point is closer to `p_A` than `1.1×` the club's distance in the same frame. The ball always leaves faster than the club;
- **pre-existing mover**: one frame before the first support point, a ball-scale peak with `z ≥ 0.6·z_first` lay within `max(2 r_A, 1.5·step)` of it. Peaks at the hypothesis's own predicted previous position or on the club head do not count. Hands, gloves and arms moving smoothly through impact fail this; a launched ball cannot;
- **single-sighting** (fast face-on exits) fails any of: `z ≥ 7`; club chain present; `d_ball/d_club` in the same frame in `[1.1, 2.2]` (smash factor plus club deceleration); ball direction within `[−12°, +60°]` of the club's impact direction, measured toward image-up (loft cone).

**Null launches (self-calibrated false-alarm level).** Re-run the identical search:
- at two distant fake anchors (±0.22 W at address height), with the winner's support removed from the peak lists;
- at two fake impact times, 12 and 20 frames earlier (pre-impact swing motion).

Accept the winner only if `S ≥ 1.15·max(S_null) + 1`. This replaces v2's inert `NULL_SCORE = 0`.

**Output:** ≤3 `LaunchCandidate`s per event, each `{t_i, φ, p_A, dir, v, ρ, support[], S, S_null, clubTrack, single}`. These seed pass 2.

Launch is **provisional**. Section 6 adds flight confirmation: the track must leave the swing envelope or exit the frame.

**View notes.**
- **Down-the-line:** usually many supporting frames. The `ρ` term carries the perspective deceleration (the scratch DTL ball moved 88, 177 and 235 px in frames 1–3, which constant velocity cannot fit).
- **Face-on:** often 1–3 frames before exit, so the single-sighting rules and the club tracker carry the decision. Real-time face-on is the hardest stratum (section 10).
- **Slow motion:** all windows are in frames and all motion is relative to `r_A`, so nothing changes; more frames per real second make every test stronger.
- **Tees and divots:** tees are slower than the club (ratio < 1.1) and tumble. Divot spray is larger, darker, chromatic and irregular (fails size, polarity and chroma). Both are in the synthetic confounder set.

Development of the scratch detector showed four ingredients were each necessary:
- local CFAR;
- the address-hole mask;
- the deceleration/shrink coupling;
- the club tracker with an extent-sized exclusion.

Removing any one let the golfer's body, the hole's side-lobes, or the club win.

Invisible-ball controls (section 10) show that the launch stage alone does not reliably abstain. Flight confirmation (section 6) is the backstop.

## 4. Flight tracking of a faint, small ball

### 4.1 State

A hypothesis `h` holds:
- its launch;
- observations `{t, x, y, σ, z, r̂, polarity}`;
- a predictor:
  - the launch model `d(τ)` plus a `½·g_img·τ²` term, until 6 observations spanning ≥ 8% of `D` exist;
  - after that, the projected-3-D fit (section 5), warm-started and refit every 4 accepted observations or when an innovation exceeds 3σ.

Prediction covariance is Laplace (`(JᵀWJ + priors)⁻¹`) propagated by numeric Jacobian, plus process noise `σ_q·(t − t_last)^1.5`.

### 4.2 ROI planning (native pixels)

- Gate: `(p − p̂)ᵀ Σ⁻¹ (p − p̂) ≤ 13.8` (χ²₂ at 0.999), plus a margin of `2 r̂ + blur`. The ROI is its bounding box, clamped to `[32, 384]` px.
- If the gate needs more than 384 px, process at ½ or ¼ scale while the predicted radius at that scale stays ≥ 0.7 px; otherwise cap the ROI and flag `gate_clipped`.
- Overlapping ROIs merge. With Source A, ROIs are indexed straight out of the `copyTo` buffer (0.03 ms per 256² crop measured); with Source B, ROIs are requested crops.

### 4.3 Evidence per ROI

- **Registration:** global camera from pass 1, interpolated to `t_k`. On textured ROIs, refine locally by block matching (±2 px, ¼-px steps, ball disc excluded); skip on smooth sky.
- **Background:**
  - median of the hypothesis' ring crops at lags `L, 2L, 3L`, with `L = clamp(ceil(2.5 r̂/|v̂|), 1, 8)` frames, so the ball has moved ≥2.5 radii per lag;
  - if `L > 8` (slow apex, slow motion), fall back to a spatial background, Gaussian `σ = 4 r̂ + 2`;
  - static specks, net knots, poles and flags therefore produce no residual.
- **Residual** keeps its sign. Polarity is learned per hypothesis (prior: address polarity), and the opposite polarity is allowed at a 0.5 z-penalty because backlit balls on bright overcast sky turn dark.
- **Matched filter:**
  - DoG at `σ = max(0.6, 0.8 r̂)` combined with a 0.7 px PSF;
  - if the streak length `ε̂·|v̂| ≥ 1.5 px`, an oriented streak kernel (3–5 shifted DoGs along `v̂`), where the exposure fraction `ε̂` is estimated from launch streaks (default 0.1).
- **Normalisation:** local CFAR z. The 41×41 (81×81 while coasting) z-map is cached as `uint8(8z)`, together with the top 6 peaks in the gate.

### 4.4 Local track-before-detect

- **Window:** `W = 6` frames; if `|v̂| < 2 r̂` per frame (slow), `W = clamp(round(12 r̂/|v̂|), 4, 12)`.
- **Search:** over endpoint offsets (0.5 px grid in the gate) and velocity perturbations (7×7 grid within ±3σ_v), score `S_tbd = Σ_{i<W} z_{k−i}(p_k + δ − i(v̂ + δv)) / √W` with bilinear sampling.
- **Acceptance threshold** `τ_tbd` is calibrated for a 2% per-frame gate false-alarm rate while tracking, and 0.2% while coasting or re-acquiring. A codec-class table comes from noise-only synthetic ROIs (Exp B: 4.4 uncompressed; 4.5–6.7 under phone-like H.264/HEVC). An online cross-check runs the same TBD in 4 displaced "null gates" at ±4σ.
- A single-frame peak with `z ≥ 6` nearest the prediction is accepted directly.
- **Observation:** a 2-D Gaussian fit on the residual gives the sub-pixel position, with `σ_obs ≈ 1.5·(r̂ + 0.7)/z`, floored at 0.15 px.

Exp B quantifies the gain. For a sub-pixel ball on sky (PSF σ 0.7 px, noise σ 1.5) the detection probability at 2% gate false-alarm rate was:

| Bitrate / codec (phone-like ≈0.25 bpp) | flux 16 single / TBD | flux 24 single / TBD | flux 32 single / TBD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uncompressed | 49% / 100% | 90% / 100% | 100% / 100% |
| x264 1 Mbps (256²@60) | 13% / 15% | 57% / **88%** | 90% / 99% |
| x264 2 Mbps | 21% / **66%** | 65% / **90%** | 90% / 96% |
| VideoToolbox H.264 1 Mbps | 8% / 8% | 44% / **85%** | 80% / 100% |
| VideoToolbox HEVC 1 Mbps | 15% / **49%** | 57% / **96%** | 89% / 100% |
| x264 CRF 23 / 28 | 0% (erased) | 0% | 0% |

Flux is `π r² × contrast` in grey levels; a white ball on blue sky has about 87 levels of luma contrast. So TBD extends DTL observation:
- 1080p, blue sky: to `r ≈ 0.30 px`, about 2.0–2.1 s for a driver (single-frame detection reaches `r ≈ 0.34 px`, about 1.8 s);
- 4K, blue sky: essentially to landing;
- 1080p overcast (contrast ~20): about 0.8 s.

Compression, not resolution alone, is the real limit.

### 4.5 Hypothesis management, occlusion, frame exit

- **Spawning and caps:**
  - each launch candidate spawns one hypothesis;
  - an ambiguous association (two acceptable peaks whose score ratio is < 1.5) spawns a child, at most 2 per frame;
  - quotas: ≤6 live hypotheses per event and ≤12 total. Events never compete for slots, so a later real launch cannot be evicted by earlier wrong ones (fixes v2-global-I3, `global-associate.js:233–244`).
- **Score** is a log-likelihood ratio versus clutter: `Σ_obs ln p(z|ball)/p(z|clutter) − Σ_miss −ln(1 − P_d(t)) − (χ²_fit − dof)/2`. `P_d(t)` comes from the predicted flux and the local-noise map, so frames where the ball should be invisible cost nothing. Length is not rewarded (v2's `log1p(n)` steal mechanism is gone).
- **Merging:** hypotheses sharing ≥70% of their observations merge, keeping the higher score.
- **Occlusion** (net, pole, tree line, cloud edge): coast on the physics prediction with no observations. Re-acquisition needs `τ_tbd + 1.5` plus velocity agreement `|v_obs − v̂| ≤ 3σ_v`, taken from the best TBD path.
- **Frame exit:** stop when the prediction leaves the frame, record `exitT` and the edge point, and hand over to completion.
- **Lost ball:** stop when `P_d < 0.05` for W frames, or when the gate exceeds 384 px at ¼ scale.

### 4.6 Identity steals

| Steal | Defence |
|---|---|
| Static speck, net knot, flag, pole | Temporal background residual is zero; the novelty test rejects persistent peaks. |
| Airplane (slow, straight, constant velocity) | Velocity gate from the TBD path; the global refit's robust weights; a terminal **residual-trend test** (≥4 trailing observations with same-signed residuals and mean beyond `2σ/√N`) truncates the steal. |
| Bird (erratic, flapping) | Fails TBD constant-velocity coherence and physics gating. Pass-1 registry of independently moving objects adds a penalty when an observation coincides with a registered track's prediction. |
| Other golfer's ball | Different anchor and launch; would need to match the gate, the velocity and the physics fit. |
| Sky noise after the ball fades (v2 "coaster") | There is no coaster. Coasting produces **predicted**, never observed, points, and coasting acceptance uses the 0.2% false-alarm threshold. Exp B calibration: about 0.24 expected false points over a 2 s 60 fps coast, each still subject to IRLS. |

### 4.7 Camera motion

Pass 1 estimates a per-frame similarity transform (translation plus small rotation and scale):
- 3-level pyramid of L1 luma;
- 20×12 grid of 32² blocks, SAD search (±8 px at the coarsest level), parabolic sub-pixel refinement;
- blocks inside the swing envelope and textureless blocks rejected;
- Tukey IRLS fit; cumulative transform to the impact frame.

Output is `camera[k] = {t, x, y}` normalised, plus optional `{s, r}` that renderers may ignore. Observations and fits live in stabilised coordinates. Pans beyond 15% of the frame raise `camera_motion_large`, because translation-only stabilisation biases the 3-D fit.

### 4.8 Sky versus ground and slow motion

- **Sky versus ground:** all thresholds are local-CFAR. Ground and tree clutter raise local σ and lower z without any per-region constants. A pass-1 horizon estimate (column-wise top boundary of the smooth bright region, robust line fit) sets `P_d` priors and the DTL landing line.
- **Slow motion:** `L`, `W`, gates and acceptance are expressed in predicted pixels per frame and `r̂`, never in seconds. The physics fit returns the slow-motion factor (section 5.2).

### 4.9 Global association (no decode)

After the forward pass, each surviving hypothesis is reprocessed:

1. Robust physics refit on the forward observations.
2. **Viterbi** over frames `[t_i, t_end]`:
   - states: local maxima of the cached z-maps inside the refit gate (much tighter than the forward gate), plus a miss state;
   - emission: calibrated z log-likelihood ratio; miss cost `−ln(1 − P_d)`;
   - transition: deviation from the refit's predicted displacement, in σ.
3. Refit and iterate 2–3 times (EM-like).

This recovers faint early-flight and post-occlusion points that the forward pass skipped, and drops forward points that the global fit rejects. Frames whose cached window does not cover the refit path are marked `not_evaluated`.

## 5. Trajectory model, smoothing and full-arc completion

### 5.1 Model

A projected 3-D golf-ball flight in camera coordinates (x right, y down, z forward).

- **Address:** the ball starts at the back-projected address point at depth 1 (unit = address depth).
- **Parameters:** `θ = (pitch, roll, ln g_eff, V0x, V0y, V0z, ln k_d, ln k_L, spin-tilt β)`.
- **Dynamics:**

  ```
  v̇ = g_eff·ĝ − k_d|v|v + k_L|v|(ŝ × v),   ṗ = v
  ```

  - `ĝ` comes from pitch and roll;
  - `ŝ` is the backspin axis, ⟂ to the launch direction and up, tilted by `β` (side spin, curve);
  - integration is RK4 with `N = clamp(160·T, 40, 1200)` steps.
- **Projection:** `f_nom = 0.8 × long edge` (phone 1× lens), with MAP model selection over `{0.8, 1.6}× long edge` for telephoto clips.
- **Observations:** image position, plus log-radius (σ 0.25) whenever `r ≥ 1.2 px`. The shrink rate is what pins `V0z` early in DTL.

**Priors are dimensionless, so they are invariant to slow motion and to `f`:**
- launch elevation versus the ground plane: N(16°, 10°);
- pitch: N(0, 8.6°); roll: N(0, 3°); tilt: N(0, 20°);
- drag `γ_d = k_d|V0|²/g_eff`: logN(ln 2.0, 0.45);
- lift `γ_L = k_L|V0|²/g_eff`: logN(ln 1.0, 0.6).

The time scale is a multi-start hypothesis `g_eff = g_nom/s²` with `s ∈ {1, 4, 8}` (prior 0.85/0.075/0.075), where `g_nom = 9.81 / Z_A`, `Z_A = f·0.02135/r_A` in metres.

### 5.2 Fitting

- Levenberg–Marquardt (numeric Jacobian in scratch; analytic projection Jacobian in the product).
- 3 IRLS rounds with Cauchy weights at `c = 2.5σ`.
- 24 multi-starts: depth ratio `{0, 0.8, 2, 5}` × pitch `{0, −0.1}` × `s {1, 4, 8}`, early-abandoned when a start's cost exceeds 3× the best.
- Outputs:
  - params and covariance;
  - `χ²/dof` and view class (dtl, face-on, oblique);
  - `slowMoFactor = sqrt(g_nom/g_eff)`; Exp A2 estimated 3.70 for true 4× and 6.4 for true 8×;
  - landing time (height above the launch plane returns to 0);
  - per-point weights. Weight < 0.1 marks an `outlier` (kept in diagnostics, not drawn). A terminal run of ≥3 outliers, or a positive residual-trend test, truncates the track as a steal.

### 5.3 Display curve (keeps every inlier observation)

```
display(t) = physics(t) + s(t)
```

- `s` is a cubic smoothing spline (Reinsch) of the 2-D residuals with weights `w_i/σ_i²`.
- It is pinned to 0 at the anchor `(p_A, t_i)` by a heavy pseudo-observation.
- `λ` is chosen by the discrepancy principle (weighted residual χ² ≈ N_eff).
- Tests assert that every inlier lies within `max(1.5σ_i, 1 px)` of the curve. This fixes the interior-point loss in `curve.js` `polishFlight/denoiseFlight` (lines 3–21, 76–101).

After the last observation, the residual correction decays as `s(t_last)·b(u) + s'(t_last)·(t − t_last)·b(u)`, with `b(u) = 1 − 3u² + 2u³` and `u = (t − t_last)/T_b`, `T_b = max(0.15 s, 6 frames)`. The drawn arc is C¹-continuous from observed to predicted: one line.

### 5.4 Ending the arc, per view

Predicted points are generated from `t_last` at 120 Hz until the first of these:

1. **Landing:** height above the launch plane ≤ 0 and predictive `σ ≤ 3%` of H. This is typical for DTL and oblique views, where the landing point is near the horizon. It ends with a small landing ring shown for 0.5 s.
2. **Frame exit:** the prediction leaves the image. This is typical face-on; the stroke runs to the edge.
3. **Fade:** predictive σ exceeds 2.5% of H, or the apparent radius drops below 0.08 px (lost in the distance). Alpha ramps to 0 over the last 15% of the predicted segment.
4. **Cap:** `t_i + 9 s × slowMoFactor`.

Predictive σ is the Laplace covariance inflated by `κ·(t − t_last)·|v_last|`. `κ` is calibrated on the synthetic tune set so that 80% of completion errors fall within 1.28σ.

**Clip ends before the landing** (common on phones): predicted points carry `revealT`. Predicted times beyond `clipEnd − 0.4 s` are compressed linearly into `[clipEnd − 0.4, clipEnd − 0.05]`, so the full arc is drawn on the last frames, TV-style. `completion.compressedReveal = true`. This can be turned off with a setting ("Finish the arc before the clip ends", on by default).

**Exp A and A2 evidence** (completion error in % of frame height):

| Observed portion | Mean error, median / p90 | End-point error, median / p90 |
|---|---|---|
| Observed until the 1080p-like detection floor `r ≥ 0.45 px` (median ≈1.35 s observed, 61 observations) | **0.2–0.3% / 0.6–0.7%** | 0.5–0.8% / 1.5–2.1% |
| Same, with `f` misspecified by ×0.7 or ×1.4 | 0.3% / 0.7% | 0.8% / 1.6–2.1% |
| Mismatch set (wind ±6 m/s, spin decay 5–25 s, topped/no-lift, strong slice, camera jitter), 1.0 s observed | 0.4% / 1.3% | 0.8% / 3.2% (max 7%) |
| Mismatch set, 0.5 s observed | 1.9% / 9.9% | 4.7% / 23% |
| Mismatch set, 0.25 s observed | 2.6% / 35% | 6.7% / 60% |
| 4× slow motion, 1 s real observed | 0.5% / 2.1% | 1.3% / 5.8% |

Baselines on the same data:
- per-axis quadratic extrapolation: 85–92% mean error (unusable);
- repo `extendFlight`: about 2% error but covers only 25–31% of the remaining flight.

Hence the uncertainty-limited fade: with less than about 0.5 s observed, the arc must fade early instead of drawing a confident wrong landing.

### 5.5 Visual treatment

The trace is one continuous stroke in the user's colour, width and glow.
- Predicted segment: same style by default (TV-like). An optional "Mark predicted part" setting draws it at 70% alpha with a dashed glow.
- Head dot: follows the observed or predicted position and shrinks to 70% size while predicted.
- Nothing is drawn before `t_i`. v2 started 0.2 s early on the parked ball (v2-frames-I8); v3's first point is the anchor at impact.

## 6. Abstention and confidence

**The legacy-link fallback is deleted.** `tracker.js:633–651` goes, so `resultGlobal` returns its own result. v3 never substitutes another engine's track. When v3 abstains, the UI says so and offers manual adjust, which must actually receive clicks (section 8, U7, CSS fix).

**`tracked`** (full arc) requires all of:
1. **Launch confirmed:** section 3.3 acceptance including null launches. The address must stay empty for ≥ `max(9 frames, 0.3 s)`.
2. **Flight confirmed:**
   - ≥4 observations, or the single-sighting face-on rule with a predicted exit;
   - flight log-likelihood ratio ≥ `τ_F` (calibrated);
   - fit `χ²/dof ≤ 3`;
   - fitted launch angle, `γ_d` and `γ_L` inside the 99% prior region;
   - **swing-disc escape:** at least one observation lies outside the swing envelope, or the track exits the frame.
     - The envelope is the union of motion pixels over `[t_v − 1.5 s, t_v + 0.5 s]`, after removing the candidate track's own `2r` footprints, dilated by `2 r_A`.
     - When the club track is measured on both sides of impact, the envelope is also bounded by the club-arc conic (circle face-on, ellipse DTL) scaled by 1.15.
     - Golfer parts, club and hands, including follow-through hands that leave a pre-impact-only envelope, never leave this union. That is what lets abstention survive launch-stage false alarms (Exp C controls).
     - The test is evaluated on the **tracked flight**, not inside the launch window. Scratch tried it on the launch-window support alone and it removed true launches (DTL 30 fps: 13/16 → 8/16; DTL 8×: 8/8 → 2/8) without fixing the controls. During the first ~10 frames the ball is legitimately still inside the swing region.
3. **Identity margin:** best hypothesis minus the best alternative with different identity (sharing < 50% of observations) ≥ 4 nats. Sibling branches are not rivals, which fixes the false `ambiguous_launch` (v2-tracker-I4).

**`partial`:** launch and flight are confirmed, but the completion faded early (`completion_uncertain`) or the identity margin is 2–4 nats (`identity_margin_low`). The observed part plus a short faded continuation is drawn.

**`no_reliable_track`:** anything else. New reason codes:
`no_address_ball`, `no_impact`, `launch_not_confirmed`, `club_only_motion`, `ball_exits_too_fast`, `flight_too_short`, `identity_conflict`, `completion_uncertain`, `slow_decode_path`, `camera_motion_large`, `multiple_shots`. The existing codes are kept.

**Confidence** is `{value, calibratedOn:'synthetic-tune-v1', components:{launch, flight, identity, completion}}`. `value` comes from a logistic model over `[S − 1.15·S_null, LLR_flight, N_obs, observed fraction of flight, χ²/dof, σ_pred at end, identity margin]`. It is fit on synthetic tune outcomes, where "correct" means identity correct and completion mean error ≤ 3% H. The bar shows `value` with "calibrated on synthetic clips". This replaces the saturating 0.90/0.95 display score (v2-ui-export-I3).

**Export and bulk:**
- export is enabled when status is `tracked` or `partial`;
- bulk mode auto-exports only `tracked` with `value ≥ 0.7`; `partial` clips are listed for review.

**Manual points** keep `status:'manual'` and are drawn with a centripetal Catmull-Rom curve **through** each point. They are never relabelled as automatic (v2-ui-export-I6), and arc completion is never applied to them.

## 7. Frame timing

- **Source A:** PTS from `stts`, `ctts` and the `elst` media-time shift, in seconds; display order is sorted by PTS. Frame duration comes from successive PTS, and variable frame rate (VFR) is supported.
  - **Timeline alignment check:** once per clip, the main thread seeks the `<video>` to the centre of frame 10 and reads the rVFC `mediaTime`. If it differs from the demux PTS by more than `Δ/4`, the offset is stored as `timelineOffset` and applied to rendering times.
  - **Rotation** must be verified against ffprobe in tests: in scratch the matrix decoded as −90 for ffmpeg's `display_rotation 90`, so the sign convention needs pinning.
- **Source B:**
  - fps comes from demux when the container parses even though the codec does not decode; otherwise from a 0.5 s muted rVFC probe (median `mediaTime` delta).
  - Seek targets are frame centres `PTS_i + Δ/2`. Each sample uses its rVFC `mediaTime`; a duplicate triggers one retry at `+Δ/4`. Without rVFC (old Safari), `tSource:'target'` is recorded and the duplicate count is reported.
  - **Measured in Chrome 155:**

    | Seek target | Clip | Duplicates | Wrong frames |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `i/fps` (v2) | 1080p30 | 30/90 | 31/90 |
    | `i/fps` (v2) | 4K60 | 40/120 | 80/120 |
    | Frame centre | 1080p30 | 0/90 | 0 |
    | Frame centre | 4K60 | 0/120 | 0 |

    With frame-centre seeks, rVFC `mediaTime` matched the barcode-decoded frame in 330/330 cases (three clips).
- **Invariance of every gate:**

  | Kind | Expressed in |
  |---|---|
  | Persistence and windows | `max(frames, seconds)` pairs |
  | Lags, TBD window, tolerances, gates | predicted px/frame and `r̂`/`r_A` |
  | Evidence thresholds | CFAR z |
  | Launch geometry | ratios (smash, arrival speed) and angles relative to the club path |
  | Physics priors | dimensionless groups |

  No constant assumes 30 Hz or 1280 px. The explicit `1/30` in `tracker.js:499` and the v2 per-sample gates are not used by v3.

  Nothing depends on handedness or left/right direction. Angles are measured relative to the club path or the fitted gravity direction, so v2's +x-referenced hook rule asymmetry (tracker-detection-I3) cannot recur.

## 9. Synthetic benchmark

### 9.1 Generator (`tests/synth/`, Node, no dependencies; ffmpeg for encoding)

**Camera.**
- Pinhole with `f ∈ {0.36, 0.8, 1.6}×` long edge (0.5×/1×/2× lenses).
- Sizes: 1080×1920 portrait (60%), 2160×3840 portrait (25%), 1920×1080 landscape (15%).
- DTL: 2–5 m behind the ball at 0.8–1.7 m height. Face-on: 2.5–8 m. Oblique: 45°.
- Pitch ±10°, roll ±3°.
- Handheld shake: random-walk plus 1–5 Hz tremor at 0.05–0.3°; optional slow pan following the ball; rolling-shutter row skew.

**World.**
- Procedural sky: clear blue gradient, haze, bright overcast (low or negative ball contrast), broken clouds (fBm), dusk.
- Tree line (fractal silhouette with texture); grass with mowing stripes; hitting mat; range markers, flags and poles; netting (thin grid with posts).
- Static lens specks.

**Golfer.**
- Articulated capsule body with clothing colours (white shirt, gloves and shoes as confounders) and a cast shadow.
- Swing kinematics: address, backswing 0.8–1.0 s, transition, downswing 0.25–0.3 s, impact, follow-through.
- Club head 35–50 m/s on a tilted swing-plane circle (hub at the lead shoulder) with a shaft.
- Driver: dark head with a small glint. Irons: metallic head with bright specular glints.
- Optional divot spray (irons from grass) and a tee that flies or stays.

**Ball.**
- Sphere radius 21.35 mm, shaded, with atmospheric haze attenuation.
- Flight from an **independent, richer** model: Reynolds- and spin-dependent `C_d`/`C_L`, spin decay, wind, slight rolling-shutter distortion.
- Launch 25–75 m/s at 4–40°, azimuth ±8°, spin tilt ±30°.
- Motion blur by temporal supersampling over the exposure (1/30 to 1/2000 s).

**Distractors.**
- Birds (erratic, flapping, dark or bright), airplanes (slow, straight, sometimes near the apex).
- Other people walking; a second golfer in the background with their own ball flight.
- Practice swings; multiple balls on the mat; a ball nudged at address.

**Sensor, ISP and codec.**
- Shot and read noise, PSF blur, sharpening halo, gamma, auto-exposure drift.
- Encoding: VideoToolbox H.264 and HEVC (phone-like, 0.1–0.3 bpp), x264 CRF 16–24; holdout adds x265 and VP9 WebM (Source B path).
- MOV with a rotation display matrix for portrait HEVC.

**Slow motion.**
- Rendered at 120/240 fps real time and stored on a 30 fps timeline. Exposure ≤ 1/240 s. Audio removed or stretched.

**Audio.**
- Impact click: broadband transient with 2–5 kHz resonance, 5–10 ms, A/V offset 0–80 ms.
- Ambient wind and voices; distractor clicks from other bays.

**Ground truth per frame** (mid-exposure time):
- ball image position and radius;
- visibility class: in frame, occluded, and a **post-compression visibility oracle**, the matched-filter SNR measured on the *decoded* frame at the true position;
- club-head position and size;
- distractor positions;
- PTS; impact time and address;
- the full projected arc to landing, including off-frame portions; landing or exit time and point; slow-motion factor.

**Speed.** Static layers are rendered once at 1.25× frame size and resampled per frame with the camera shake; only dynamic objects are supersampled. The target is ~50 ms per 1080p frame, with clips rendered in parallel processes (14 cores).

## 10. Risks, what synthetic tests cannot prove, and limiting overfitting

**Hardest stratum: face-on at real-time frame rates.** In a close portrait face-on view the ball crosses the frame in 1–2 frames at 30/60 fps (Exp A0: exits after 2–3 frames at 60 fps). Exp C shows D2 rarely picks the wrong object there but mostly abstains (`ball_exits_too_fast`). Mitigations:
- single-sighting rules with the club tracker;
- streak-orientation evidence (a blurred streak must point back to the address);
- suggesting slow motion or a wider shot.

Targets are set honestly lower for this stratum.

**Invisible-ball false launches.** Controls re-render the scene with the ball never drawn after impact (`expC-controls.txt`). The launch stage alone abstained:

| Stratum | Abstained |
|---|---|
| DTL real time | 31/32 |
| Face-on real time | 28/32 |
| DTL 8× slow motion | 6/16 |
| Face-on 8× slow motion | 8/16 |

An earlier control that painted background pixels along the hidden ball path left a ball-like trail; that was a flaw in the control, not the detector.

The slow-motion false launches are smooth hand, arm and club arcs coherent over 4–8 frames. Two rules target them (section 3.3): outrun-the-club and pre-existing mover. Scratch result: slow-motion control abstention rose from 14/32 to 10/16 on a smaller rerun, real-time controls stayed at 14/16, and slow-motion positives went to 32/32 correct with 0 wrong. The rules help but do not close the gap on their own. Flight-level confirmation (swing-disc escape, physics fit, identity margin) is the second line of defence and **was not validated in scratch**; U3, U4 and U8 must test it on these exact controls.

**Short observation.** Under 0.5 s observed, completion has fat tails (p90 10–35% H). The uncertainty-limited fade is the safety valve; `κ` calibration is synthetic-only.

**Compression.** Phone encoders differ from x264 and VideoToolbox. Exp B shows CRF 23–28 erases faint balls, so real recall could be far below synthetic recall if real clips are encoded more harshly. The visibility oracle prevents synthetic over-claiming, but cannot predict real encoders.

**Synthetic-to-real gap.** The following are not modelled faithfully and are not proven by these tests:
- real ISP denoising that smears sub-pixel balls; HDR/10-bit (P010) frames; EIS warps (non-rigid); real club-glint statistics;
- real golfers and clothing; real sky texture; phone frame-interpolated "fake slow motion" (ghost balls); variable frame-rate quirks;
- edit-list and timeline offsets in the wild; real audio (crowded ranges with many clicks).

Synthetic tests cannot establish real recall, precision or completion accuracy. They can establish mechanism correctness, invariances (fps, scale, slow motion), timing integrity, performance, and the absence of known failure modes.

**Platform.**
- WebCodecs availability and HEVC support vary by browser and OS; unsupported configurations use Source B.
- `copyTo` may be slower on phones with GPU-backed frames.
- Fragmented MP4 from some Android apps falls to Source B until a `moof` parser is added.
- Memory pressure on low-end phones: the governor lowers L1 and stride.

**Physics mismatch.** Mishits (shanks, tops), ground rollers, strong wind, slopes. The fitted priors could produce a plausible but wrong descent. Mitigation: dimensionless priors are broad; fade when uncertain; predicted points are marked and reported, never counted as observations.

**Limiting overfitting to the generator.**
1. CFAR, z and ratio thresholds instead of absolute grey levels.
2. A self-calibrated null (null launches, null TBD gates) adapts to each clip.
3. The generator's physics differs from the fitter's.
4. A style-pack holdout with out-of-distribution factors, run once per release candidate and logged.
5. A tuned-parameter budget: every tuned constant lives in `dist/v3/params.js`, mirrored in `TRACKING_V3_DESIGN.md` with its tune value; adding one requires a decision row.
6. Mutation tests per unit, so tests pin behaviour; the legacy tracker's gates had 21/22 surviving single-rule mutants.
7. Adversarial confounders in both splits.
8. Report tune versus holdout side by side; a holdout regression larger than 1.5× blocks.
9. The next step after synthetic acceptance is a small labelled real-clip set (contact sheets already exist in `tests/contact-sheet.mjs`) before claiming real-world accuracy. Until then the UI says "calibrated on synthetic clips".
